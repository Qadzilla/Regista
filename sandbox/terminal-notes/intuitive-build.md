# The intuitive round: the Scout terminal, less Bloomberg

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

Zaid liked everything the terminal does but found its naming and layout "a bit too Bloomberg". This round makes the 15
changes he approved. It changes only the sandbox (`sandbox/terminal.js`, `terminal.css`, and the terminal markup in
`index.html`). It is a mock for him to approve before anything is ported to the real app. Every capability is still
there: commands, panels, linking, evidence that plays, saved layouts, definitions on hover, the noisy mark, the synthetic
label and keyboard use.

## The 15 changes, before and after

### Naming

1. **Search box.** Before: `CMD>` in mono yellow, a mnemonic placeholder (`TOURE · RANK FW npxg · CMP · …`) and a `<GO>`
   button. After: a normal search box with a magnifier, the placeholder "Search a player, club or stat…", the ⌘K hint
   and a yellow **Search** button. Typed commands still work, in plain words (`rank forwards goals`, `compare Toure
   and Ustinov`, `plot npxG against xA`, `similar to Toure`, `who plays like Toure?`, `touch map Toure`, `shots …`,
   `form …`, `radar …`, `team Calder Town`) and as the old mnemonics (`RANK FW npxg`, `CMP`, `SCAT`, `SIM`, `SHOT`,
   `FORM`, `HEAT`, `RDR`, `TEAM`, `HELP`). Suggestions are labelled Command, Player, Club, Stat and Position, and stats
   go in by name, not by key. The status line answers in sentences, for example "Forwards ranked by Non-penalty xG: 18.
   Top: Femi Toure, 1.15." instead of "RANK FW npxg: …".
2. **Panel buttons.** Before: `F1 SCRN Screener · F2 PLYR · F3 RDR · F4 CLIP · F5 SHOT · F6 HEAT · F7 FORM · F8 SCAT ·
   F9 TEAM`. After: Players, Player, Radar, Clips, Shots, Touch map, Form, Scatter, Team, Help. Each button's tooltip
   gives its shortcut ("Players: go to it (Alt+1)"), and `aria-keyshortcuts` says it too. Alt+1 to Alt+9 still work.
   A hidden panel's button is dashed and says "is hidden: show it". Panel headings are the same plain names.
3. **Help.** Before: a table of mnemonics with `<GO>`. After: "How to use the Scout terminal" lists plain words, each
   with an example you can click: a name, a club or a stat, rank, compare, plot, similar to, shots, form, touch map,
   radar and team. One line says the short forms still work (RANK, CMP, SCAT, SIM and the rest). The keys section
   explains Alt+1–9 as "in the order of the panel buttons".
4. **Saved layouts.** Before: `WS 1 2 3 Reset`. After: "Saved layouts: 1 2 3" and "Reset layout", each with a tooltip.
   Messages say "Saved layout 2." and "Layout 1 reset to the default."
5. **Selection.** Before: `FOCUS Femi Toure · CMP none · + add Toure`. After: "Selected: Femi Toure BRO · FW · #9"
   with a **Compare with…** button. It fills the search box with "compare Femi Toure, " and suggests players; picking
   one runs the comparison. While comparing, the line reads "Comparing: [Toure ×] [Ustinov ×] Compare with… Clear".
   If the selected player is not in the comparison, the button becomes "Add <name> to the comparison" (it names who it
   replaces when three are already there). The player card has the same Compare with… button.
6. **Wording.** "Pctl" became "Percentile". "Qualified (101+ min)" became "Regulars only (101+ minutes)": the engine's
   threshold is 100.5, shown rounded up. "QUALIFIED" on the card became "Regular". "Include unqualified" became
   "Include non-regulars". Tooltips say "percentile among regular forwards". The ≈ mark has a one-line legend in the
   header, "≈ small sample, read with care". It opens a short note in plain English: the sample size, what ≈ means,
   how percentiles work, and what a number opens. The technical export rules are no longer shown there. Tooltips call
   it "≈ Small sample, read with care" with the split-half r, and its accessible name is "small sample, read with care".
7. **Panel controls.** Before: `◀ ▶ L S ×` always shown as mono letters. After: SVG icons for move earlier, move later,
   size and close. They appear on hover and whenever anything in the panel has keyboard focus, and they are always
   shown on touch screens. Each has a tooltip ("Size: small (click for medium)") and an accessible name ("Form: small,
   make it medium"). An arrow that cannot move is disabled. Column headers lost their "▼" and "×". A click sorts and
   a second click reverses. The sorted column is yellow with a yellow underline, and the panel header says, for
   example, "Sorted by NP goals, highest first". Removing a column moved into a **Columns (8)** menu, which also has
   "Add a stat…" and "Default columns". The menu stays open while you remove several columns, and it closes on Escape
   or on a click outside it.

### Placement

8. **Order.** Before: the screener with Clips beside it, then player, radar, form, then the pitch charts, scatter and
   team. After: the search with the selected player beside it on top. Below that, the players table on the left; the
   player card and the radar are stacked on its right, and the table runs the full height of both, scrolling inside
   itself. Then form, shots and touch map; then scatter and team. The table only runs that height on screens wider than
   1100 px, and only while it is first, large, and followed by two small panels. Otherwise it falls back to the normal
   grid.
9. **Clips drawer.** Before: a permanent Clips panel saying "Pick any number…". After: no Clips panel. A number opens a
   drawer on the right (a bottom sheet on phones) with a clear **Close** button. The first number moves keyboard focus
   to the drawer's heading. Escape or Close brings focus back to the number, or to the same number after a repaint.
   While the drawer is open, later numbers only change what it plays, so you can keep arrowing through the table. The
   Clips panel button (Alt+4) opens and closes the drawer. The empty message only shows when you open the drawer
   yourself.
10. **One header line.** Before: four rows (command line, function keys, meta strip with the SYNTHETIC DATA badge, and
    the FOCUS/CMP strip). After: the search and the selection share one row; the examples and the status line come
    next; then one compact line with the panel buttons on the left and the ≈ legend and saved layouts on the right. The
    synthetic-data note moved to the bottom of the section and is said once, plainly: "Synthetic data: invented players
    and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista. Clips play from …".
11. **Short column labels.** The table, the radar axes and the panel context use short labels: "NP goals", "NP xG",
    "NP xG/shot", "Prog. passes received", "Tkl + int (adj.)", "Passes into box", "Def. third pass %" and so on (a
    small map plus rules). Labels of 12 characters or fewer never wrap; longer ones wrap to at most two lines. The full
    name, definition, rule and source stay in the tooltip on hover and focus, and the full name is the header's
    accessible name.

### Interaction

12. **Clickable numbers.** Every number now has a solid underline. On hover or focus the underline turns yellow, a
    small ▶ appears, and the tooltip ends with "▶ 18 moments · click to play" (or "· Enter plays them" in the table).
    Physical numbers say "matches". Team numbers count their shots or matches. The count is the full total, and the
    drawer says "First 10 of 18" with a link to the rest. Accessible names say "play its 18 moments".
13. **Example chips.** Before: the mnemonic placeholder. After: "Try" with **Best finishers** (`rank forwards
    non-penalty goals`), **Compare Toure and Ustinov** (`compare Femi Toure and Amari Ustinov`) and **Who plays like
    Toure?** (`similar to Femi Toure`). Each chip's tooltip shows the command it runs. A chip keeps focus after it runs,
    so a phone does not pop up its keyboard.
14. **Rows and profile.** A click anywhere on a table row selects the player. The name and the numbers still do their
    own jobs, and the row shows a pointer. The player card has an **Open profile →** link to the sandbox's player page
    (`#scout/player/<id>`). The sandbox only has pages for the 66 players in `scout.json`, so for the rest the link reads
    "Open profile on the live app ↗".
15. **Phone panel row.** At 720 px and below, a sticky row of the panels sits under the site header and the view tabs.
    Each tap brings that panel's top up under the row; the panel in view is yellow, and Clips opens the bottom sheet.
    The function-key row is hidden on phones, except Help. The site's sticky view tabs now stay above the table's sticky
    header while the terminal is open (they were painted over before).

## Saved layouts: version 3

Layouts are saved as `v: 3`, and older ones load as follows:
- A v2 layout that was still the v2 default takes the new default.
- A changed v2 layout keeps its order, sizes and hidden panels, minus Clips, which is now a drawer.
- v1 is handled as before.
- Saved players, comparison, columns, sort, filters and history carry over.

I checked this in the browser with a stored v2 default and a stored v2 custom layout.

## Checked

I served the sandbox on 127.0.0.1:8191 (now stopped) and checked it at 1440 and 375 px:
- The page does not scroll sideways at either width.
- Every example chip works, and so do these 23 typed commands, old and new: `RANK FW npxg`, `rank midfielders by key
  passes`, `CMP TOURE USTINOV`, `compare … and …`, `SCAT npxg xa FW`, `plot progressive passes against key passes
  midfielders`, `plot xT added by passes against xa`, `SIM`, `who plays like Toure?`, `similar to`, `touch map`,
  `SHOT`/`shots`, `FORM`, `radar`/`RDR`, `TEAM BRO`, `team Calder Town`, `BRO`, `goals`, `Toure`, `help`, plus an
  unknown word.
- The drawer opens from the table, the card, form bars, shots and team numbers. Clips play from the live app. Escape
  and Close return focus.
- Also checked: the Columns menu (remove, stays open, closes outside), sorting and its header text, row click, Compare
  with… then a pick, move/size/hide and show again, Alt+4, the tooltips with "▶ N moments", the phone row's jumps and
  highlight, and the bottom sheet.
- The console showed no errors from the page. The only errors were from my own test script.

Two limits of that check:
- The browser pane was hidden during testing, so it ran smooth scrolls and scroll events slowly. I checked the jump
  positions with instant scrolling. The phone row marks the panel you tapped straight away, so it does not depend on
  scroll events.
- I did not test with a screen reader.

## For the port

There, "Open profile" should link to `/players/{id}` for everyone.
