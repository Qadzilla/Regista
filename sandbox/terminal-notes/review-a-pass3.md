## Should fix

1. **A FORM bar can say "N in this match" when the match has more moments** (`evidenceReel`, terminal.js lines 352-358,
   and the "in this match" count at line 442).
   - **The cause:** the export keeps the first 10 evidence items per number. When a bar's match straddles that cut, the
     match filter keeps only the items before it. The panel then reports them as the whole match. The "past the first
     N exported" note appears only when none survive.
   - **Example:** FORM Femi Toure, non-penalty xG, R5 bar.
     - The bar reads 0.85 per 90, about 0.285 xG.
     - CLIP says "1 in this match" and lists one shot of 0.044.
     - The engine has three shots in that match: 0.044, 0.064 and 0.176.
   - **Scale:** 3,069 evidence lists are capped. In a random 300 of them, 222 had a straddling match with more events than
     shown. That projects to about 2,300 player, metric and match bars that undercount.
   - **Fix:** when `total > shown` and the match is the last shown item's match, say "first K of this match (the export
     keeps the first 10 of N)" and link to the full list on the local app.
2. **The radar overlay draws a missing percentile as the 0th** (`renderRadar`, `ptsOf`, line 630; the outline added in
   round 2 makes this common).
   - **The bug:** in CMP mode a metric with no percentile is plotted at the inner ring. The dashed focus outline does the
     same.
   - **Example 1:** select a goalkeeper during a forward comparison (Jonah Eriksen while comparing Toure, A. Ustinov and
     Reyes). The dashed outline collapses to an octagon on the inner ring. It reads as 0th percentile on all eight
     forward metrics, but none of them is scored for keepers.
   - **Example 2:** `CMP Femi Toure, Farid Okafor`. Okafor's touches in the box and progressive passes received are "–" in
     the table, yet his polygon dips to the centre on both.
   - This was a round 1 nit. Round 2's outline turns it into a false reading every time the focus is of another position.
   - **Fix:** break the polygon, or mark the axis as "no percentile", for null percentiles. When the outsider's position
     is not the template's, say so in the note ("not scored for goalkeepers on the FW template") instead of drawing it.
