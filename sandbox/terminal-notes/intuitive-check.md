# The intuitive round: independent check

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

This check covers the build in commit a286a59 (`intuitive-build.md`). I served the sandbox on 127.0.0.1:8192 and used my own
browser tab at 1440x900 and at 375x812 (the phone preset). Afterwards I reset the viewport, cleared the test storage and
stopped the server. The browser pane was hidden, which slows smooth scrolling and focus timers, so I waited before reading
scroll positions. I did not test with a screen reader.

## The 15 changes: all present

1. Search box. The placeholder reads "Search a player, club or stat…", next to a ⌘K hint and a yellow Search button. No
   CMD> or <GO> is left.
2. Panel buttons. Players, Player, Radar, Clips, Shots, Touch map, Form, Scatter, Team and Help. Each tooltip gives its key
   ("Players: go to it (Alt+1)") and `aria-keyshortcuts` says the same. Alt+4 opens and closes the drawer; Alt+7 and
   Alt+8 move to their panel and focus its heading.
3. Help lists the commands in plain words, each with an example you can click. One line lists the short forms (RANK, CMP,
   SCAT, SIM, SHOT, FORM, HEAT, RDR, TEAM, HELP).
4. "Saved layouts: 1 2 3" and "Reset layout".
5. "Selected: Femi Toure BRO · FW · #9" with "Compare with…". While comparing, the line shows the chips, Clear, and "Add …
   to the comparison".
6. The wording reads Percentile, "Regulars only (101+ minutes)", "Include non-regulars" and the card tag "Regular". The
   header legend "≈ small sample, read with care" opens a short note.
7. The move, size and close icons appear on hover and on focus (their opacity is 0.05 at rest). Each has a tooltip and an
   accessible name, for example "Players: large, make it full width", and an arrow that cannot move is disabled. Headers
   have no ▼ or ×. The Columns menu removes a column and stays open.
8. Order at 1440: the players table on the left, with the player card and the radar stacked on its right. Below them come
   form, shots, touch map, scatter and team.
9. There is no Clips panel. A number opens the drawer on the right (a bottom sheet at 375), focus goes to its heading, and
   Escape or Close brings focus back to the same number.
10. The header is one compact line, and the synthetic-data note appears once, at the bottom ("Synthetic data" occurs once
    in the page text outside Help).
11. Column headers use short labels with the full name, definition, rule and source in the tooltip on hover and on focus.
    Several of them wrapped to three lines, which is fixed below.
12. Numbers are underlined. On hover a ▶ mark appears and the tooltip ends "▶ 5 moments · click to play". Accessible names
    say "Play its 5 moments".
13. The example chips are Best finishers (rank forwards non-penalty goals), Compare Toure and Ustinov (compare Femi Toure
    and Amari Ustinov) and Who plays like Toure? (similar to Femi Toure). All three ran correctly.
14. A click on a row selects the player. "Open profile →" goes to `#scout/player/<id>`, which shows that player's page,
    and Back returns to the terminal. For a player missing from scout.json (Milan Novak) the card reads "Open profile on
    the live app ↗", which opens in a new tab with noopener.
15. Phone: a sticky row of the panels. A tap on Radar brought the radar panel up just under the row (8 px below it) and
    marked Radar as current. On phones only Help stays in the panel-button row.

## Nothing lost

- **Typed commands.** All of these gave the right answer: TOURE, RANK FW npxg, CMP Toure Ustinov, SCAT npxg xa FW, SIM,
  SHOT, FORM, HEAT, RDR, TEAM BRO, HELP, and a bad input ("Nothing found for "xyzzy qq"…"). So did the plain-word forms
  (rank midfielders by key passes, compare Toure and Ustinov, plot npxG against xA, similar to Toure, who plays like
  Ustinov?, touch map / shots / form / radar <name>, team Calder Town, BRO, goals). There were no exceptions.
- **Search box.** Typing "ust" suggests five labelled Players plus one Stat. Arrow keys and Enter pick a suggestion. ↑
  recalls earlier searches in order. ⌘K and Ctrl+K focus the search box.
- **Linked selection.** A selection from a scatter dot, a "Plays like" player, a squad player, the radar legend, a
  comparison chip or a table row updates the card, form and the other panels.
- **Evidence.** Numbers open the drawer from the card, the radar table, the form bars, the shots, the touch map and the
  team numbers. A clip played from the live app: the first request took about 7 s while the clip rendered, then it
  played at 1280 px wide, and "Play all" moved on to the next clip.
- **Keyboard only.** In the table, arrow keys move between cells. Enter opens the drawer with focus on its heading. Tab
  reaches Close and Enter on it returns focus to the cell. Escape does the same.
- **Saved layouts.** Save, reload and reset all work. Switching layouts restores each one's panels, players and columns.
  A hidden panel's button reads "Touch map is hidden: show it (Alt+6)" and brings the panel back.
- **Old saved layouts.** I stored a version-2 store from before this change, with layout 1 at the old default and layout
  2 changed (team M first, screener XL, radar and heat hidden, and a comparison). It loaded as follows: layout 1 took the
  new default; layout 2 kept its order, sizes and hidden panels without Clips; the comparison and history carried over.
- **Definitions.** They show on hover and on keyboard focus of a header. The ≈ legend opens its note.
- **Widths.** Neither 1440 nor 375 scrolls sideways (scrollWidth equals the viewport).
- **Console.** No errors in the console or on `window.onerror`, in the terminal or on any other sandbox route (each was
  visited once: home, profile pages, scout pages, search and all 66 player pages).

## Fixed in this check

1. **Headers wrapped to three lines** ("Shot- / creating / actions", "Touches / in the / box", "Prog. passes / received"),
   which change 11 was meant to stop. The table squeezed each label down to its longest word. A wrapping header button now
   has a 92 px floor. I measured all 86 long short labels at that width with the ≈ mark: every one fits on two lines.
2. **A big empty box beside the radar.** The tall players panel stretched to the full height of the card plus the radar
   (1547 px), but the default forwards list has 18 rows, so about 900 px of the panel stayed empty. Now the panel is only
   as tall as its content, capped at the height of the card plus the radar, and longer lists scroll inside it. With all
   102 players it caps at 1547 px and scrolls; the form row does not move. The cap is measured in terminal.js, with a
   ResizeObserver plus a measure after each paint and resize, since a hidden tab holds observer callbacks back.
3. **"Compare with…" suggested the selected player first.** Picking the top suggestion gave "Compare needs two or three
   different players." Comparison suggestions now leave out players already named before the last ",", "and" or "vs".
4. **"Add Ustinov to the comparison" was ambiguous** with Amari Ustinov already in the comparison and Otis Ustinov
   selected. The button now says the full name, "Add Otis Ustinov to the comparison", and names the full name it replaces
   when three are already there.
5. **The ≈ note used words this round removed.** It said "a qualified player's season" and "a metric marked noisy", and
   named a code module ("regista.season.reliability"). It is shown now as "a regular's season", "a stat marked ≈" and
   without the module name, in the legend and in Help. The export text in terminal.json is unchanged.
6. **xA moments read "xG 1" each.** The export gives xA's moments (the key passes) a value of 1 each, with no xA per pass,
   so the drawer listed "xG 1" seven times for Toure's 0.418 xA. Those rows now show no value. This was there before this
   round, and the export should carry each pass's xA (see below).

## Not fixed (for Zaid or the port)

- **"Ustinov" on its own picks Otis Ustinov, a defender.** So do `CMP Toure Ustinov` and `compare Toure and Ustinov`,
  because a surname resolves to the player with the most minutes. The example chip is right, because it uses full names,
  but typing the chip's own words gives a different comparison. It would help to prefer the selected player's position,
  or players with stats on the current radar, when names tie. This was there before this round.
- **Sorting by a removed column.** Removing the column the table is sorted by leaves the sort on it, so the header says
  "Sorted by NP goals" with that column gone. This was there before this round.
- **The phone header is tall.** At 375 px, the search, selection, examples, status, Help, legend and saved layouts take
  about 430 px before the panel row. Saved layouts could fold into a menu on phones.
- **A gap in the left column on wide screens.** After fix 2, a short list leaves page background under the players table
  beside the radar instead of an empty panel. It is cleaner, but still a gap.
- **Tooltips after a tap on the phone row.** In the emulator, the scroll can leave the pointer over a radar slice and show
  its tooltip. Real touch screens have no hover, so this is probably only the emulator.
- **Export, for the port.** Each xA moment should carry its pass's xA, and the sample note should say "regular" in the
  export itself.
- **Radar axis labels** still shorten after 18 characters ("Prog. passes rece…"), as the build noted.
- **Not tested with a screen reader.**
