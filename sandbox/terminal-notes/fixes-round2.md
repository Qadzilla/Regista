# Scout terminal: fixes for the round 2 reviews (part B6)

All 8 findings fixed and none rejected. terminal.json did not need a re-export: the team shot reels are built from the
shots already in it. Checked in a browser at 1440x900 and 375x812 on port 8175, against part A's local app on :8040.

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No-percentile reason wrong | A new `noPct(p, m, c)` follows the engine's own rule (metrics.py `_percentiles`): a season count ("a season count, ranked by value only"), not scored for the position ("not scored for defenders (scored for MF FW)"), under the qualifying minutes, below the minimum attempts ("2 attempts, needs 4"), or the only qualified peer (Bruno Lindqvist, aerial win %). The tooltip, the screener's aria-label and its italic small-sample style use it; only minutes and attempts get the italics now. |
| 2 | FORM mixes units for counts | For the 9 count metrics the dashed line is the season count divided by matches played, labelled "season 0.90 a match (9 in 10)" (Jonah Eriksen, clean sheets; y axis to 1). The caption names the unit per kind: count per match, per 90, the match's own ratio, peak, mean. |
| 3 | Team-style units and "no clip" | "/90" marks only the per-90 rows; the header is "Style"; ratio rows say "the match's value" and the sub-line shows the unit ("11.6 passes"). xG, non-penalty xG, penalty xG, goals and shots, for and against, now open every shot behind them with its clip, from the export's shot records. For all 6 clubs the shots add up to the engine's totals exactly (counts) or within 0.01 (xG), and the note says so. Set-piece shots open the set-piece shots. Pressures and high turnovers say their events are not listed. |
| 4 | Screener ALL ranks disagree with the engine | Ranked rows are now the engine's pool: a value and `qualified` for that metric (minutes, and attempts for a ratio), whatever the position; season counts rank whenever they have a value. Percentile tie-breaks apply only within one position. Checked all 127 metrics x (ALL, GK, DF, MF, FW) = 635 pairs against `/api/terminal/rank`: 635 match, 0 rank cells differ. RANK ALL big_chances puts Farid Okafor 12th at 0.30; the RANK line says how many are unranked and why. |
| 5 | Panels open below the fold | `showPanel` now tests the panel's chart or table (or the part the command is about: the SIM list, the player card) with `inViewFit(..., 0.6)` and scrolls so the panel top sits under the bar, or the target's own top when the panel is too tall. From the top of the page, every command (CMP, RDR, FORM, SIM, SHOT, HEAT, SCAT, TEAM, RANK, a name) leaves its target 100% in view at 1440x900 and at 375x812. TEAM, SCAT and RANK repaint before they scroll. |
| 6 | RANK sorts by a hidden column | RANK moves the ranked column first and resets the table's scroll; on a phone it also scrolls the table (not the page) until the column shows whole. At 375, RANK FW npxg reads 0.67, 0.64, 0.40, 0.33, 0.27 in the first visible metric column. |
| 7 | Grid tooltip covers the next rows | Arrowing through the screener shows a short tip (metric, the player's value and percentile or the reason, "the column header has the definition") beside the cell, right or left; above the row only when neither side has room. Placed again after the cell scrolls into view. Checked with real arrow keys at 1440. |
| 8 | RDR ignores the focus during CMP | When the focus is not one of the compared players, it is drawn as a dashed white outline, with a dashed legend entry, the panel context "CMP + Fontaine", and a line: "Comparing 3; Haruki Fontaine (the focus) is the dashed outline, not in the table. + add (replaces Toure)". |
