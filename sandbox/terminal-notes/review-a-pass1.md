# Review A, round 1: are the terminal's numbers right and honest?

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

**Verdict: not signed off.** One thing must be fixed and five should be. The export itself is exact. The problems are in
how `terminal.js` draws, orders or labels some numbers.

Scope: commit 39a15c2 on `ux/sandbox`. I served the sandbox on 127.0.0.1:8173 and ran part A's local app on :8040 with
`REGISTA_DOTENV=` and nothing sent to Azure. I fixed nothing. The 8173 server is stopped and the test tab is closed.
The commands I typed (RANK DF dribbled_past, RANK FW shot_on_target_pct) are still saved in WS1 in that origin's
localStorage.

## Must fix

1. **FORM draws negative values off the chart** (`renderForm`). The y scale runs from 0 to max(values) only.
   - Six metrics have negative values: `xt_added`, `xt_passes`, `xt_carries`, `np_goals_minus_xg`, `onpitch_xg_diff`,
     `onpitch_goals_diff`.
   - For Toure's xT added by passes, 9 of 10 matches are negative. Their bars become 0.5 px slivers with y up to 777
     inside a 178-high viewBox.
   - Their value labels and the "season −0.234" line land outside the chart and are drawn over the consistency table
     below it.
   - The panel therefore shows one positive bar and hides the season value. Draw a zero baseline inside the range
     [min(0, lo), max(0, hi)].

## Should fix

2. **Ratio evidence lists each moment twice and does not say why** (export plus `evidenceReel`).
   - For a ratio, the engine's evidence merges the numerator and denominator events, and tags every item with its
     `metric`. The export keeps only `{m, v}`.
   - Example: Toure's Shots on target % reads "8 of 19" but lists "First 10 of 27". Each on-target shot appears twice
     ("R1 v EAS 1'" four times for two shots), with no value and no label.
   - npxG per shot gives "of 38" for 19 shots, and pass completion gives "of 206" for 117 passes.
   - Fix: export the item's metric, then label each row (on target / attempt) or merge them by event.
3. **Screener ties are ordered the wrong way round** (`screenRows`).
   - Exported values are rounded, so equal values fall back to a tie-break of more minutes first.
   - For a per-90 metric sorted best first, that is the reverse of the true rate. RANK FW np_goals shows Okafor (p69),
     Whitfield (p75), then Tanaka (p81), numbered 4, 5, 6.
   - The engine's `/api/terminal/rank` puts Tanaka, Whitfield, Okafor all at rank 4.
   - RANK's output line likewise names one "top" player where two are tied (dribbled_past: Okafor and Vargas are both
     rank 1).
   - Fix: break ties by percentile, or by total/minutes, in the sort direction, and number tied rows the same.
4. **The heat map says "361 touches" right under "Touches 171 tot"** (`renderHeat`).
   - The grid counts `TOUCH_MAP_EVENTS`: touches, passes, crosses, carries, take-ons, shots, tackles, interceptions,
     recoveries and clearances. The Touches metric counts touch records only.
   - The caption and each cell's `<title>` should say "on-ball actions" (or list them), not "touches".
5. **"Rank 1 = highest" is false for the three lower-is-better team metrics** (`renderTeam` caption).
   - The engine ranks xG against, goals against and shots against best first. BRO's 0.98 xG against is rank 1, the
     lowest in the league.
   - Say "Rank 1 = best (for style markers, the highest)".
6. **Match minutes are one behind Regista's own clip labels** (export and every list).
   - The terminal shows the clock's minute field: 1:19 reads "1'".
   - Regista's clip labels use the football minute (`clips.match_minute`, 1-based). The same clip is titled "Femi Toure
     (9): shot at 2'", and a 6:33 shot appears as 6' against "shot at 7'".
   - Moments in the first minute show "0'".
   - Fix: show minute + 1 (45 + … in the second half, as `match_minute` does), or show mm:ss.

## Nits

- **Radar overlay:** a missing percentile is drawn at the centre (0), which reads as worst. The table shows "–".
  Leave a gap or mark it.
- **SIM:** 26 of 510 similar entries are negative. Hugo Fontaine's "most similar" is −0.06, drawn as a 2% bar. Say "no
  close match" when the similarity is ≤ 0.
- **SHOT:** the header says "BRO · 20" and the map has 20 dots, while Shots reads 19 and on target 8. The legend
  explains it (19 non-penalty, 1 penalty) because FBref Sh excludes penalties. A short "penalty shown, not counted"
  would make it plain.
- **FORM caption** says "Per 90 of each match". For the 9 total-kind metrics (cards, penalties, clean sheets) the bars
  are counts per match.
- **TEAM:** the header says "Style (per 90)", and team per-match evidence notes say "per 90 of the match". Neither is
  true for the 7 ratio metrics (possession %, PPDA, …).
- **TEAM ties:** penalty xG is 0.00 for five clubs, ranked 2 to 6. The engine does this; the terminal could show the
  ranks as tied.
- **walking_share:** CLIP's subtitle shows "12135.6 of 17989.3" with no unit (seconds).
- **CLIP:** the header shows no ≈ mark for a noisy metric, and a clip plays from its start rather than from the moment.
  For example, the shot at 79.55 s sits 10.6 s into a clip that starts at 68.95 s.
- **Help:** it repeats the synthetic sentence (`D.note`), so it appears twice while help is open. It also says "Every
  number opens the moments behind it", but SD, CV, heat cells and team style values open per-match values or nothing.
  The panels say so themselves.
- **Checker's open items, confirmed:** the namesake choice and the unitless m and km/h values in RANK output and
  screener cells.
