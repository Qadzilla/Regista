# The intuitive round: fixes for review pass 2

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

This round fixes the four "should fix" findings from `intuitive-review-pass2.md`. I checked the result in a browser on
127.0.0.1:8194 at 1440x900, 1024x800 and 375x812. There were no console errors. Afterwards I cleared the test storage,
reset the viewport, closed the tab and stopped the server.

## 1. Empty space on wide screens

- **The layout.** The table now sits top left, with the card and radar in a right column beside it and an empty slot
  under the table. The page order is now table, card, radar, then the rest, so Tab follows the order of change 8. This
  also fixes the nit that Tab reached Form and Shots before the card.
- **`fitTall`.** It used to move panels up "while they fit". Now it tries every count: none, then the first panel after
  the radar, then the first two, and so on up to six. It keeps the count that leaves the least empty space on the
  page. That counts the space beside the radar, the half row a single panel leaves in the slot, and the rows below.
- **`fillRows`.** This is new. Below the columns, each row of panels widens to fill all 12 columns. A panel's size is
  how much of a row it asks for. Whatever the row leaves over is shared among its panels. This works at every width
  above phone size, including 1024 and layouts with panels hidden.
- **Measured at 1440 (default, forwards).** Form, Shots, Touch map and Scatter now sit under the table, two to a row.
  Team spans the full width below. The only gap left is about 395x210 px under the radar, down from three blocks
  (790x280, 395x490 and 395x650).
- **Measured at 1440, other lists.** With goalkeepers, the gap is about 790x75 px. With every position, the tall table
  keeps the panels below it, in full rows (4+4+4, then 4+8).
- **One trade-off.** A panel alone in its row now fills the row. So a size works as a minimum: "small" asks for a third
  of a row.

## 2. Common phrasings

`natural()` now handles these:

- **Openings.** It strips as many opening words as there are: "who has", "who's" (read as "who is"), "which players",
  "show me", "open", "pull up", "look up", "search for", "go to", "can you", "play", "watch" and "see".
- **Verbs after "who".** The verb names the stat: "who scores the most" ranks goals, and "who creates the most chances"
  ranks shot-creating actions.
- **"per 90".** It ignores "per 90", "per game", "p90" and "/90".
- **Club filters.** It reads "at / for / from / in <club>", or a club as the first word ("Brookfield best finishers"),
  as a club filter. The club goes into the table's own filter box, where it shows and can be cleared. The status line
  says "Forwards at Brookfield Rovers ranked by …". Only a strict club name counts, so "in midfield" is never read as
  a club.
- **Bare names.** Once the openings are gone, it tries a bare player, then a bare club. "Show me Toure" and
  "open Toure" open the player.

Each of the reviewer's eight examples now works.

## 3. Highlights

- **The command.** `highlights <player>` is new; `HL` also works. "highlights", "clips", "moments", "videos" and "reel"
  work as the first word. "best moments" and "highlight reel" also work. "Toure highlights", "Toure clips",
  "Toure's best moments", "watch Toure" and "play Toure highlights" all reach it. "highlights" on its own plays the
  selected player's.
- **The reel.** It opens one reel in the drawer:
  - goals, assists and saves first
  - then every other shot, from the shot list, with its xG and outcome
  - then chances made, nutmegs, skill moves, take-ons won, tackles won, interceptions and blocks
- **No repeats.** Each moment appears once, match by match within each kind.
- **The status line.** It says what is playing, for example: "Playing Femi Toure's highlights: 5 goals, 13 other
  shots, 5 chances made and 2 interceptions (25 moments)."
- **Elsewhere.** Help lists the command with an example, and the player card has a Highlights button.
- **Checked.** The first clip loaded from the live app. A goalkeeper gets his saves: Hugo Fontaine has 7. A player with
  nothing gets a plain "no highlights to play yet".

## 4. The phone table

- **Columns.** At 720 px and below, the Club and Pos columns hide. Club, position and role show as a small second line
  under the name. The rank number is pinned to the left, so a name that wraps stays tidy.
- **Measured at 375.** Player is 0–132 px, Mins 132–170 and NP goals 170–240 of the table's 325 px. Femi Toure's 1.64
  shows whole on the first screen. The page never scrolls sideways.
