# The intuitive round: independent review, pass 2

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

This review covers commit f73d519, which fixes the nine findings from pass 1. I served the sandbox on 127.0.0.1:8193 and
tested in my own browser tab at 1440x900, 1024x800 and 375x812. I used real clicks for the main route and scripted
searches for about 70 typed questions. I changed no code. Afterwards I cleared the test storage, reset the viewport,
closed the tab and stopped the server. The browser pane was hidden, so I waited for each screenshot to update. I did not
test with a screen reader. There were no console errors.

The question: could a football scout who has never seen a Bloomberg terminal understand this at first sight, and get from
"who are the best finishers?" to watching the moments behind a number without help?

**Verdict: not signed off.** The main route now works for a newcomer, whether they type or click. "who are the best
finishers?" ranks the forwards. The status line reads "Top: Femi Toure, 1.64 per 90". Clicking 1.64 opens the drawer,
the clip plays and Play all moves on to the next one. Escape or Close returns focus to the number. Most of pass 1's fixes
hold up. Four things should still be fixed, and the first one is a fix that did not fully work.

## Should fix

1. **The empty space on wide screens moved instead of going away.** At 1440 with the default layout, Form and Shots now
   sit under the players table. But a gap of about 790x280 px is left under them, beside the radar. Below the columns,
   Touch map and Scatter fill only two thirds of their row, leaving an empty block about 395x490 px on the right. Team
   (8 of 12 columns) leaves another empty block about 395x650 px beside it. A short list (goalkeepers) moves four panels
   up and still leaves Team alone with an empty third. Measured panel tops: table 215, Form and Shots 820, card 215,
   radar 989, Touch map and Scatter 1696, Team 2195. Fix: make the panels below the columns fill their rows, for example
   Touch map and Scatter at half width each and Team at full width.
2. **Common ways to phrase a question still fail.** Each of these answered "Nothing found": `who has the most goals?`,
   `who has the most assists?`, `who's the best finisher`, `who scores the most`, `show me Toure`, `open Toure`,
   `goals per 90` and `best finishers at Brookfield`. Shorter versions work (`most assists`, `who is the best finisher?`).
   The opening words that natural() strips do not include "who has", "who's" (after "'s" is removed it reads "who the")
   or "who scores". After "show me" or "open" is stripped, a bare player name is never tried. Fix: strip those openings,
   fall back to a player or club once they are stripped, ignore "per 90", and read "at <club>" as a club filter.
3. **"Toure highlights", "Toure clips" and "Toure moments" find nothing.** In a product built around clips, this is how
   people will ask to watch a player. Today it reaches only a stat ("Toure goals"). Fix: map highlights, clips, moments,
   videos and best moments to a reel for that player, such as his goals and shots, or his best moments if the export has
   them. Say on the status line what is playing.
4. **On a phone, the stat the scout ranked by is cut off on the first screen.** At 375 the sticky Player column (150 px),
   Club, Pos and Mins take 302 of the table's 325 px. Only 48 of NP goals' 70 px show, so "1.64" appears as "1.6" with no
   other stat visible, and you have to scroll sideways to see any number you searched for. Fix: on phones, show the club
   and position as a small second line under the name, or hide them, so the sorted stat shows in full.

## Nits

- After a mouse click on a number, keyboard focus in the table can stop in two places. When the drawer closes, focus
  returns to the number, which has tabindex -1, while the name cell keeps tabindex 0. After one arrow key, two cells
  have tabindex 0.
- A tooltip opened by keyboard focus stays at its old place on the screen after the page scrolls (by design, while the
  cell keeps focus). It then floats over unrelated panels.
- "best defenders" ranks by progressive passes, the first defender column. A scout expects tackles and interceptions, or
  aerial duels. "top scorers" ranks goals per 90, not season goals. The status line says "per 90", which helps.
- "Toure vs Ustinov" lists the other Toures ("could also be") but not the other Ustinovs.
- On a phone the players panel header is cut: "Sorted by NP goals, hig…".
- A Team panel 0.00 (Fairhaven, Penalty xG) still looks like a link and "opens the matches behind it".
- On wide screens, once Form and Shots have moved up, Tab goes from the table to Form and Shots before the player card.
  The reading order no longer matches change 8's "card and radar beside the table".
- Still there from pass 1: tooltip jargon ("Rule FBref G-PK", "split-half r 0.073"), Help's heading "How to use the
  Scout terminal", and columns that pile up as each search adds its stat.
