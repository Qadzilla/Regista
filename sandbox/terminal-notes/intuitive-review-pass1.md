# The intuitive round: independent review, pass 1

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

This review covers commit b402b02 (the build in a286a59 and the check's six fixes). I served the sandbox on 127.0.0.1:8193
and used my own browser tab at 1440x900 and at 375x812. Afterwards I cleared the test storage, reset the viewport, closed
the tab and stopped the server. I changed no code. The browser pane was hidden, so I drove the page with scripted clicks
and key events. I did not test with a screen reader.

The question I asked: could a football scout who has never seen a Bloomberg terminal understand this at first sight, and get
from "who are the best finishers?" to watching the moments behind a number without help?

**Verdict: not signed off.** All 15 changes are there, and nothing from before is lost. The route works when you click:
the Best finishers chip, then Femi Toure's 1.64, then the drawer plays his 5 goals, and Escape returns focus to the number.
But the search box now looks like an ordinary search, and it fails on the plain questions that look invites, including the
first chip's own label. Four more things should be fixed before Zaid sees it.

## Should fix

1. **The search box fails on plain questions, including "Best finishers".** I typed these and each one answered "Nothing
   found": `who are the best finishers?`, `best finishers` (the first chip's label), `top scorers`, `best dribblers`,
   `forwards`, `strikers`, `Toure shots`, `Toure form`, `Toure goals`, `Toure vs Ustinov` and `Femi Toure vs Amari Ustinov`.
   `compare Toure with Ustinov` answered "No player called "with"". Change 1 made the box look like a normal search,
   so a scout will type questions like these. Fixes: accept each chip's label as a query, plus a few synonyms (best,
   top, finishers, scorers, dribblers, creators); accept a position word on its own as "rank that position"; accept
   `<player> <panel or stat>` and `X vs Y` without a command word; treat "with" like "and".
2. **Typing "Ustinov" picks a defender.** "Ustinov" on its own opens Otis Ustinov (DF). So `compare Toure and Ustinov`,
   typed exactly as the chip reads, compares Toure with a defender, while the chip compares him with Amari Ustinov (FW).
   The check found this too. It was there before this round, but now the chip teaches that exact phrase. Prefer a
   namesake in the selected player's position, or the player in the current table.
3. **The table never says its numbers are per 90.** After Best finishers the table and the status line both say "Femi
   Toure, 1.64", and the player card says "Goals 1.64". He has 5 goals, so a scout can read 1.64 as a total. "Per 90"
   appears only in tooltips and in the drawer ("1.64 goals per 90 · 5 in total"). Say it once where people read: for
   example "values per 90 minutes" in the table caption, and "1.64 per 90" in the status line.
4. **Numbers with no moments still look like play links.** A 0.00 cell (Femi Toure, Assists) is underlined, shows the
   ▶ on hover, and its tooltip says "▶ 0 moments · click to play". Its accessible name is "Play its 0 moments". Clicking
   it opens a drawer that says "0 moments", with Prev, Next and Play all that do nothing. About half the forwards' cells
   in the default view are 0.00. A zero should look like plain text, or the drawer should say plainly that there is
   nothing to play.
5. **On a wide screen, a big empty block sits beside the radar.** At 1440 the players table runs from y 215 to 812, while
   the card and radar on its right run to 1648. That leaves an empty area 792 px wide and about 840 px tall. When you
   scroll to the radar (for example after a compare), the left half of the screen is blank. The check noted this gap
   but did not fix it. Fill it, for example by moving form, or shots, up under the table when the table is short.
6. **On a phone the first screen shows no data.** At 375x812 the first table row starts at y 815, just below the fold.
   Before it come about 490 px of header (search, selection, examples, status, Help, legend, saved layouts, panel row),
   then 270 px of table controls (position, filter, regulars, value/percentile, columns, a three-line caption). With a
   comparison running, the selection line wraps to three rows. Fold saved layouts and the legend into a menu on phones,
   keep the chips on one row that scrolls sideways, and shorten the caption.
7. **"Saved layout 2." is said when you switch, not when you save.** Clicking 2 shows the message "Saved layout 2." A
   scout reads that as "I just saved my screen into 2", but the click loads slot 2 (empty, so the default). There is no
   save action: the current layout saves itself. Say "Layout 2 (changes save automatically)", or "Switched to layout 2".
8. **Removing the sorted column leaves the table sorted by it.** I removed NP goals from the Columns menu. The panel
   still said "Sorted by NP goals, highest first" and kept that order, with no visible column to explain it and no
   header to click back. The check found this too; it was there before this round, but change 7 moved removing into
   the menu. Fall back to sorting by the first remaining column, or say so.
9. **Radar labels are still cut.** "Prog. passes rece…" and "Shot-creating act…". Change 11 promised short labels; give
   these two shorter labels (for example "Prog. received" and "Shot-creating"), with the full name on hover.

## Nits

- The panel buttons keep the old order (Players, Player, Radar, Clips, Shots, Touch map, Form, Scatter, Team), but the
  page now goes Form, Shots, Touch map, and the phone row puts Clips last. Alt+5 to Alt+7 follow the buttons, not the page.
- Leftover technical words: the table caption's "ranked by value (ratios need the minimum attempts)", the legend's
  "split-half reliability within a role is below 0.3", tooltips' "split-half r", the Form panel's "Consistency: Mean SD
  CV", and the team panel's unexplained "PPDA" and "Field tilt" (their definitions are only on hover).
- Match labels differ: the drawer says "R1 v EAS" where Form says "R1 @EAS" for the same away match. "R1" for "round 1"
  is shorthand.
- Every rank adds its stat as the first column and never removes it: after three searches the table had 11 columns.
- The focused "Clips" heading in the drawer gets a yellow box and looks like a button.
- The section and its Help are still called "terminal" ("How to use the Scout terminal"), the most Bloomberg word left.
- "Compare with…" fills the box with the command "compare Femi Toure, ". It works, and it suggests the right players,
  but a list to pick from would hide the command syntax.

## Checked and fine

- **All 15 changes are there.** Search box and button; plain panel names, with shortcuts in tooltips and
  aria-keyshortcuts; Help in plain words with examples; "Saved layouts: 1 2 3" and "Reset layout"; "Selected: …" with
  "Compare with…" (the pick ran "Comparing Femi Toure and Amari Ustinov"); Percentile, "Regulars only (101+ minutes)"
  and the ≈ legend; move, size and close icons on hover and focus, with names such as "Form: small, make it medium";
  the order (table left, card and radar right); the drawer and the phone bottom sheet; one header line with the
  synthetic note at the bottom; short headers with full names as accessible names; the ▶ and "▶ 5 moments"; the
  example chips; row click selects ("Add Milan Brennan to the comparison" while comparing); "Open profile →" goes to
  `#scout/player/pl_bro_07`; the sticky phone row.
- **Evidence.** Best finishers, then Toure's 1.64, opened the drawer with focus on its heading. It read "1.64 goals per
  90 · 5 in total · 97th percentile" and listed 5 moments, and the clip played from the live app. Escape closed it and
  focus went back to the same number. At 375 the bottom sheet took the lower half of the screen, with focus inside it.
- **Commands.** `goals`, `assists`, `nutmegs`, `rank strikers goals`, `Brookfield`, `Brookfield Rovers` and `who plays
  like Ustinov` all answered. The ranked column went first.
- **Selection and comparison.** Selecting a player outside the comparison shows them dashed on the radar ("Comparing 2
  + Brennan"), and the card and form follow the selected player.
- **Saved layouts.** Switching keeps each slot's comparison and columns. The v1 and v2 migration in `clean()` reads
  correctly: an untouched default takes the new default, and a changed layout keeps its panels without Clips.
- **Code.** In the new header code (meta, examples, selection, keys, phone row), every value goes through esc().
  Storage is wrapped in try/catch.
- **No page errors** at either width. The page never scrolled sideways at 375 (its width stayed 375).
