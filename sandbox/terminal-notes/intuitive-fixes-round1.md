# The intuitive round: fixes for review pass 1

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

This round fixes all nine "should fix" findings in `intuitive-review-pass1.md`. I checked them in a browser on
127.0.0.1:8194, at 1440x900, 1024x800 and 375x812, with clean storage each time. There were no console errors, and the
page never scrolled sideways. Afterwards I cleared the test storage, closed the tab and stopped the server.

1. **The search box takes plain questions.** If a search is not a command, a name, a club or a stat, `natural()` reads
   it as a question. It drops filler ("who are the", "show me", "best", "top", "most") and maps plain words to stats:
   finishers, scorers, dribblers, creators, assisters, passers, tacklers, headers, pressers, runners, the fastest, and
   shot-stoppers. A position word ("forwards", "strikers", "in midfield") picks the position. Checked, with the answer
   each one gave:
   - `who are the best finishers?` and `best finishers` rank forwards by non-penalty goals.
   - `top scorers` ranks everyone by goals; `best dribblers` ranks everyone by take-ons won; `best dribblers in midfield`
     ranks midfielders.
   - `forwards` and `strikers` rank forwards on their first default column.
   - `Toure shots`, `Toure form`, `Toure radar` and `Toure team` open that panel. `Toure goals` plays his 5 goals.
     `Toure assists` says there is nothing to play.
   - `Toure vs Ustinov`, `Femi Toure vs Amari Ustinov`, `compare Toure with Ustinov` and `compare Toure and Ustinov` each
     compare Femi Toure with Amari Ustinov.
   - A search that still fails now suggests "best finishers" and "Toure vs Ustinov". Help has a new "a question" row
     with examples you can run.
2. **"Ustinov" means the right Ustinov.** When several players share a name, the search now picks one in this order:
   the selected player; someone the selected player plays like; when comparing, someone in the same position as the
   first player named; someone in the current table; then most minutes. The other namesakes are still offered beside the
   answer. Suggestions follow the same order, so pressing Tab on "compare Toure and Ust" picks Amari, not Otis.
3. **Per 90 is said where people read.**
   - The table caption now says, in bold, "values per 90 minutes", adding "(except %, averages and totals)" when a
     column is not per 90. In Percentile view it says "percentiles vs the same position".
   - Season-count columns are marked "(total)".
   - The status line reads "Top: Femi Toure, 1.64 per 90".
   - In the player card, the radar table and the comparison table, per-90 rows carry "/90" and season counts carry
     "total". A one-line note explains "/90".
   - Tooltips and accessible names give the value with its unit.
4. **A zero is plain text.** A number with no moments behind it has no underline, no play mark and no hand cursor. Its
   tooltip says "No moments behind this number", and its accessible name ends "No moments to play". Clicking it says so
   in the status line ("Femi Toure: Assists 0.00 per 90, so there are no moments to play"), and no drawer opens. If the
   drawer opens with nothing to play (a form bar for a match with no moments), it hides the video and the Prev, Next and
   Play all controls, and says "Nothing to play." with the reason.
5. **No empty block beside the radar.** On wide screens the table sits in a left column and the card and radar sit in a
   right column. When the table is shorter than the card and radar, `fitTall()` moves the next panels up under it, two
   to a row, in their order, while they fit. When the table grows, they move back down.
   - At 1440 with forwards: the table runs to y 812, then Form and Shots sit under it until y 1401, beside the radar,
     which ends at 1688.
   - With all players, the table fills the left column and nothing moves up.
   - At 1100 px and below, the columns dissolve (`display: contents`) and the order is unchanged.
   - Keyboard focus is kept when a panel moves. Form and Scatter are redrawn when they change width.
6. **The phone's first screen shows the table.** At 375x812 the first row now starts at y 629, down from 815. The
   changes:
   - Saved layouts and the small-sample note fold into a "Layouts & notes" button.
   - The selection and the example chips each keep to one row that scrolls sideways.
   - The table caption keeps only what its numbers are.
   - The filter box is narrower.

   While I checked this, I also fixed a page that scrolled sideways during a comparison: the header grid now has
   `minmax(0, 1fr)`.
7. **Switching layouts says what happened.** The messages are now "Switched to layout 2, which was empty, so it starts
   from the default (changes save automatically)." and "Switched to layout 1 (changes save automatically)."
8. **Removing the sorted column re-sorts the table.** The table sorts by the first column left, or by minutes if no
   columns remain, and the status line says so. This works however the column was removed: the menu, Default columns,
   or an old saved layout.
9. **Radar labels fit.** The labels are now "Prog. received", "Shot-creating" and "Goal-creating". Every axis label
   shows the full name on hover.

Not done: the review's nits (panel-button order compared with the page order, leftover technical words in the
legend, Form and Team panels, "R1 v" versus "R1 @", rank adding columns, the focus box on the drawer heading, the word
"terminal" in Help, and the command text in "Compare with…"). They are left for Zaid to decide on.
