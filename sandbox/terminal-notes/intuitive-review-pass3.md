# The intuitive round: independent review, pass 3

Synthetic data: invented players and clubs in the Regista League, generated by Regista.

This review covers commit 94006f0, which fixes the four findings from pass 2. I served the sandbox on 127.0.0.1:8193 and
tested in my own browser tab at 1440x900, 1024x800 and 375x812. I used real clicks for the main route, the drawer,
"Compare with…", the column menu, the phone sheet and the sticky panel row. I ran about 90 typed questions by script. I
also loaded a hand-made version 2 saved layout to check the migration. I changed no code. Afterwards I cleared the test
storage, reset the viewport, closed the tab and stopped the server. There were no console errors. The browser pane was
hidden, so I waited for smooth scrolls to finish before reading results. I did not test with a screen reader.

The question: could a football scout who has never seen a Bloomberg terminal understand this at first sight, and get from
"who are the best finishers?" to watching the moments behind a number without help?

**Verdict: not signed off, on one finding.** The main route works, by clicking or by typing. Click "Best finishers" or
type "who's the best finisher". The status line reads "Top: Femi Toure, 1.64 per 90". Hover 1.64 and it says
"5 moments · click to play". Click it and the drawer opens with the clip playing from the live app. Close or Escape puts
focus back on the number. All 15 changes are in, and all four of pass 2's findings are fixed. One gap in plain questions
is left, and it hits the stats scouts ask about most.

## Should fix

1. **"most <stat>" and "<stat> per 90" only work for a short list of stats.** These all answer "Nothing found":
   `most xG`, `who has the most xG`, `best xG`, `top xA`, `highest xA`, `most shots`, `who has the most shots`,
   `most nutmegs`, `who has the most nutmegs`, `most key passes`, `most interceptions`, `most progressive passes`,
   `most touches in the box`, `most minutes`, `top 5 xG`, `xG per 90`, `npxg per 90`, `best save percentage`.
   The bare stat works for nearly all of them: `xG`, `xA`, `nutmegs`, `key passes`, `progressive passes`. So does a
   position or club filter: `forwards xg`, `best forwards by xG`. So do the synonym words: `most goals`,
   `assists per 90`, `most saves`, `fastest players`.
   - **Cause** (`natural()` in sandbox/terminal.js). After the openers ("who has", "most", "best", "per 90") are
     stripped, the leftover text only goes to `synStat()` (the SYN list). `resolveMetric()` is tried only
     `if (pos || cl)`. With no position or club, nothing matches. The plain-stat fallback that makes `xG` work runs on the
     raw text, so it never sees "xg" without "per 90" or "most".
   - **Fix.** When an opener, a superlative or "per 90" was stripped, try `resolveMetric(rest)` with no position too, so
     "most xG" ranks all players by xG. When it matches several stats, offer the same "matches N stats" chips that
     `rank` gives.
   - **The same gap for single words.** `tackles` alone and `Whitfield tackles` answer "Nothing found" because "tackles"
     matches several stats. They should offer those choices too.

## Nits

- **The focus tooltip after a mouse Close.** Click a number, then click Close: focus goes back to the number (right for
  keyboard users), and its tooltip opens over the next three rows of the table. It stays there until you click
  somewhere else. Clicks pass through it (pointer-events none), but it hides the numbers a scout reads next. Show the
  focus tooltip only after keyboard input.
- **The Clips button reopens the last list.** It opens the last list played, even after another player is selected.
  For example, Femi Toure's goals came back while Amari Ustinov was selected and three players were being compared. The
  drawer names the player, so this is not wrong. But the button could say "Clips: last opened" or open the selected
  player's highlights when nothing has been opened for them. When closed, it is also drawn dimmer than the other panel
  buttons, so it looks disabled.
- **A failed search clears the box.** After "Nothing found for 'who are the best finishers at Ashford?'" the text is
  gone, so fixing a typo means retyping. Up arrow brings it back, but nothing says so. Keep the text selected on a miss.
- **Phrasings that still fail:** `who is better Toure or Ustinov` ("or" is not read as a comparison),
  `strikers like Toure` (a position before "like"), `youngest players` (age is on the card but cannot be ranked).
- **`save %` alone ranks all 66 players,** with 62 "below the minimum attempts". `most saves` correctly ranks the
  goalkeepers. A goalkeeper-only stat could default to goalkeepers.
- **Still there from earlier passes:** tooltip jargon ("Rule FBref G-PK", "Source FBref shooting table",
  "split-half r 0.073 (below 0.3)") on every number's definition, "best defenders" ranking by progressive passes, and
  "Toure vs Ustinov" not listing the other Ustinovs.
- **Wide screens:** one gap is left, about 395x212 px under the radar at 1440 with the default layout, as the fixer said.
  At 1024 the gaps are small (76 px between card and radar). Acceptable.
