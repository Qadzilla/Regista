## Should fix

1. **The no-percentile reason is wrong for about 1,500 cells** (`tipHtml`, terminal.js line 815; the screener's `uq`
   style).
   - **The bug:** every missing percentile reads "no percentile (below the minimum)". Qualified players have 1,649
     cells with a value and no percentile. Only 145 of them are below the minimum attempts.
   - **The real reasons:**
     - The metric is not scored for the player's position: 909 cells, plus 240 for unqualified players.
     - The metric has no percentiles at all: the 9 count metrics, such as clean sheets, cards and penalties.
     - The player is the only qualified peer: Lindqvist's aerial win %.
   - **Examples:**
     - Hover Farid Okafor (DF, 297 min) in `RANK ALL big_chances`: "0.30 (1 in total) · no percentile (below the
       minimum)", drawn in the italic "small sample" style. Big chances is scored for MF and FW only.
     - FORM Jonah Eriksen, clean sheets: "Jonah Eriksen 9 · no percentile (below the minimum)".
   - **Fix:** choose the text from `m.has_percentile`, `m.positions.includes(p.position)`, the attempts against
     `m.min_attempts`, and `p.qualified`.
2. **FORM mixes units for the 9 count metrics** (`renderForm`, lines 671-698 and 707).
   - **The bug:** the bars are counts per match, but the dashed "season" line is the season total. The caption says
     "Per 90 of each match".
   - **Example:** FORM Jonah Eriksen, clean sheets has ten bars at 1 or 0 under a line labelled "season 9", and the y
     axis runs to 9 (checked on screen). The line reads as if he kept 9 clean sheets a match.
   - **Fix:** for `kind === 'total'`, draw the season line at total / matches (or leave it out) and label it, and say
     "count per match" in the caption.
3. **Team-style values say things that are not true** (`teamReel` line 794-797, `renderTeam` header line 779).
   - **Wrong unit:** the per-match rows of every team metric say "per 90 of the match", including the 7 ratios. Brookfield
     Rovers shows "R1 @ EAS 57.1% per 90 of the match" and PPDA "6.9 per 90 of the match". The table header also says
     "Style (per 90)" over possession %, PPDA and the other ratios.
   - **Wrong source:** for xG for and against, goals and shots for and against, the reel note says "A team-style number is
     built from every possession of a match: one value per match, no single clip." These numbers come from shots,
     and every shot has a clip. The engine only exports shot evidence for set-piece xG.
   - **Fix:** say "per 90" only for per-90 metrics. For the shot-based metrics, say the shots are not listed for this
     number, or export them as the set-piece xG evidence already is.
4. **The ALL-positions screener disagrees with the engine and hides higher values** (`screenRows` line 446-447,
   `screenRanks` line 478).
   - **The bug:** with no position filter (the default for a bare metric such as "big chances", and for `RANK ALL …`),
     rows without a percentile sort after every ranked row, unranked. So players whose position the metric does not
     score drop to the bottom whatever their value. `/api/terminal/rank` with no position ranks all 66 qualified players
     by value.
   - **Scale:** 39 of 127 metrics differ, 934 rank cells in all.
   - **Examples:**
     - `RANK ALL big_chances` lists Farid Okafor's 0.30 below a run of forwards at 0.00. Amari Haddad is 13th in the
       screener and 14th in the engine.
     - For `walking_share` and `ball_losses` the engine's rank 1 is a goalkeeper; the terminal names someone else as
       top.
   - **Why it matters:** the output line ("66 players by Big chances; top …") reads as a ranking by value.
   - **Fix:** either rank by value as the engine does and keep the "–" only for the percentile, or say in the caption
     and output line that ALL ranks only the positions the metric is scored for.
