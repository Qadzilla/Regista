## The two fixes

### 1. "▶ N moments" now counts each event once (the number the drawer plays)

**What was wrong.** A ratio's evidence lists each event once per key. A pass is one row; if it was completed, it is a
second row. A sum lists each event once per part. The drawer merges these rows, one per event (`mergeEvidence`).
`evidence_total`, however, counted the rows. The build report says this count is "every moment the drawer will play",
which was true only for single-key stats.

**What showed it.** The table, the player card, the compare table and the scatter all said "play its 216 moments" for
Toure's 74.2% pass completion. The drawer then said "124 moments".

**The fix:**
- `SeasonMetrics.evidence_total` (`season/metrics.py`) counts distinct events, using the same key the client merges on:
  match plus event id, or match plus time for an event without an id.
- The evidence record has a new `moments` field beside `total`. `total` still counts rows, and paging still uses it.
- `terminal.js` reads `moments` for the player's full metric table (`fullFn`).
- `/api/terminal/screen` cells and scatter points carry the same number, as before.
- Docstrings are updated in `terminal_api.py`, plus one sentence in CLAUDE.md §2.10.

**How I checked it:**
- In the browser, Toure's pass completion now reads "Play its 124 moments", and the drawer lists 124.
- By script, for every metric of two players (254 numbers), `moments` equals the number of rows `mergeEvidence` would
  make.
- New test: `test_a_ratios_count_is_the_moments_the_drawer_plays_each_event_once`. It covers pass completion (124 of
  216 rows, on the screen, the full table and the scatter) and a sum (`npxg_plus_xa`). Each is checked against the
  evidence paged in full and merged the way the drawer merges it.

### 2. "most" and "highest" list the biggest numbers first

**What was wrong.** Ten stats are better when lower: ball losses, offsides, dribbled past, aerial duels lost, fouls
committed, yellow cards, red cards, goals conceded, on-pitch goals against and on-pitch xG against. For these, "most
yellow cards", "who has the most yellow cards?", "most goals conceded" and "most fouls committed" sorted lowest first.
So the answer to "who has the most cards" was 41 players tied on 0.

**The fix:**
- `terminal-words.js` marks a stat question that says most, highest, biggest, largest or greatest (`most: true` on the
  `rankKey` intent).
- `cmdRank` then sorts the biggest first and says so: "Players ranked by Yellow cards (most first; fewer is better): 66.
  Top: Noel Oliveira, 4 this season."
- "best goals conceded", "top yellow cards" and a bare "yellow cards" keep the stat's better way, lowest first, as
  before.
- Stats where higher is better are not affected.

**How I checked it.** In the browser, after a cache-bypassing reload, I ran "most yellow cards" (Noel Oliveira 4),
"most goals conceded" (2.09 per 90 first), "most fouls committed", "best goals conceded" (0.90 first) and "yellow
cards" (lowest first). New test: `test_most_of_a_stat_where_lower_is_better_lists_the_most_first`.

## Needs Zaid's decision

1. **"most" against "best" for stats where lower is better (fix 2 above).** I made "most yellow cards" mean the most
   cards, because that is what the words ask. "best" and "top" still mean the better way. If you would rather "most"
   follow the stat's better way, revert the `MOST` lines in `terminal-words.js` and the `low` line in `cmdRank`.
2. **The new `moments` field (fix 1 above)** is a small read-only addition to `/api/players/{id}/metrics` evidence. I
   added it so "▶ N moments" matches what the drawer plays. Nothing else uses it.
3. **Not changed; minor, for later:**
   - Team numbers say "▶ click to play what is behind it" without a count until that club's style or shots have
     loaded. The sandbox counted them up front from its export; the app loads them only on demand.
   - "Toure vs Ustinov" picks the namesake by the similar-players list only once Toure's similar players have loaded.
     Before that, it picked Hugo Ustinov (same position) instead of Amari. The full-name example chip is not affected.
   - Static files are served with an ETag and no `Cache-Control`. After a deploy, a browser can briefly keep an old
     `terminal.js` beside the new page. This is true of every page, not only this branch.
   - The sandbox reviewers' nits the build report lists are still open: tooltip jargon, "terminal" in Help's heading,
     columns piling up, the other Ustinovs not listed, and "youngest players".
