## Should fix

### S1. "fewest / least / lowest <stat>" answers "Nothing found"

Where: `src/regista/static/scout/terminal-words.js`, the `SUPER` and `MOST` regexes (lines 110-112) and `statIntent`.

The safeguard made "most yellow cards" mean the most cards. A scout who then asks for the other end gets "Nothing found…
Try … "most xG"…". These phrasings all failed in the browser:
- fewest yellow cards
- least fouls
- lowest xG
- fewest goals conceded
- who concedes the least
- worst finishers

The bare stat ("yellow cards", "goals conceded") already ranks the better way, so the data path is there; only the
words are missing. The suggested fix:
- read "fewest", "least", "lowest" and "smallest" off a question the way "most" is read;
- pass `fewest: true` (smallest numbers first, whichever way the stat is better) to `cmdRank`;
- add a test beside `test_most_of_a_stat_where_lower_is_better_lists_the_most_first`.

This goes slightly beyond Zaid's literal request ("most <stat>" and "<stat> per 90"). But it is the mirror of that
request, it costs very little, and a scout looking at keepers or discipline will type it.

## Nits (no change needed to sign off)

- The status punctuation differs: "Yellow cards (most first; fewer is better)" against "(lowest first: lower is
  better)".
- The "could also be" note quotes the typed case: "Toure vs Ustinov" gives `"toure" could also be: …`, while "compare
  Toure and Ustinov" gives `"Toure"`.
- "most pass completion %" offers 9 stats, because the label plus "%" is not one of the stat's names. "highest pass
  completion" and "pass %" work.
- In the table, a mouse hover's tooltip ends "· Enter plays them". This matches the sandbox, but a mouse user would
  expect "click to play".
- Team numbers never show a count in their accessible name ("Open what is behind it"); the count appears only in the
  tooltip, once the club's data has loaded. The safeguard already noted this.
- The form chart's x labels touch at 1440 (the "at DUN" and "at ASH" boxes meet at x=413). They are readable, but
  tight.
- The players table has no caption or `aria-label`. This was the same on main and in the sandbox.
- After a few searches the table had built up 22 columns. This is a known sandbox nit that Zaid has not asked for; the
  Columns menu and "Default columns" handle it.
- At 375 two texts are cut off: the "Filter by name or club" placeholder and "Sorted by NP goals, hig…".
- When the table shows every position (66 rows), it stretches to 1476 px beside the card and radar, and Form, Shots and
  Touch map move below it, three across. This is how `fitTall` is designed, but the panels jump when the row count
  changes.
- "best young players" still answers "Nothing found", because age cannot be ranked. This is known and listed in
  build.md.
