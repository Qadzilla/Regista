# Regista: the pitch

Written 2026-10-27 for the submission form. Everything named runs today on the live app or from this repository; the
evidence for each claim is in `docs/criteria.md`.

## In one line

**Regista directs every touch.** Give it a football match and it finds every moment each player is on the ball, plus
every replay they appear in: cut, labelled, explained, and found by asking.

## Short version (for a form field)

A 17-year-old winger in a Spanish youth league films his matches on a phone. A scout in England will never watch them:
nobody has logged the footage, and nobody can say how good he is compared with wingers in Morocco or Ghana at his
level. Regista changes that.
- Its vision agents find every player in the footage, and its intelligence agents cut every moment each one is on the
  ball, plus every replay, then label and recap it.
- A Foundry agent answers questions like "the best U19 dribblers up front", working through two MCP servers: Regista's
  own and Microsoft's Azure MCP Server.
- Every player is compared only with players at the same level, age and gender, and every number plays the moments it
  was counted from.

It runs as Microsoft Agent Framework workflows on Microsoft Foundry and Azure Container Apps. Uploads are processed by
an event-driven Container Apps Job. Microsoft Fabric recomputes its player numbers from the raw tables to check them. All the data is synthetic and
generated by Regista.

## The problem

Every highlight reel, replay package and scouting report starts with the same slow job: someone watches the whole match
and writes down where each player touched the ball.
- Broadcasters and creators need those moments in minutes, not hours.
- Below the professional line nobody does that job at all. Players and small clubs film their own matches, the footage
  sits unwatched, and talent is judged by who happens to be in the stands.

## What Regista does

- **Finds every moment.** Vision agents identify players by kit number, never by face. They follow the ball and spot
  passes, shots, take-ons and nutmegs. Intelligence agents then cut each player's on-ball moments and every replay or
  highlight they appear in, label them, score how watchable each is, and say why.
- **Lets anyone ask.** "His nutmegs", then "now the replays". Match recaps can be told from any player's side, and each
  sentence plays the moment it describes.
- **Builds a catalogue for recruitment.** Players and teams upload matches: 14 competitions in 10 countries, from
  semi-pro to U17 academies, men and women. Scouts filter by country and level. Every percentile names its pool ("97th
  percentile among 16 academy U19 men forwards"), small samples are flagged, and every number opens its moments.
- **One engine, four paths.**
  - Fans and creators: reels and exports.
  - Broadcasters: recaps and replay packages.
  - Scouts: a Scout view and a terminal over 127 metrics, each with its definition and source.
  - Players: upload your match and watch it join your profile.

## How it is built

- **Microsoft Agent Framework:** two workflows of distinct agents with typed handoffs, retries, dead-letter routing,
  checkpoints and a human "is this you?" step.
  - Vision: shots, identity, review, possession, events.
  - Intelligence: ingest, validation, clips, labels, review, recap, persistence, indexing, profiles, the Librarian.
- **Microsoft Foundry** (`gpt-5-mini`): the label, Librarian, Scout and recap agents. Their answers are checked against
  the numbers their tools returned, and they fall back to rules, saying so, when Foundry is unavailable.
- **MCP:** Regista's MCP server and Microsoft's **Azure MCP Server**, both called by Foundry agents from inside the
  container.
- **Azure:**
  - Container Apps serves the app.
  - A Container Apps Job, scaled by KEDA on a Storage queue, runs the vision agents on uploads stored in Blob Storage.
  - Cosmos DB holds matches, clips, labels and human verdicts, and AI Search indexes the clips.
  - Application Insights traces every agent step through Foundry tracing.
- **Microsoft Fabric:** the league and the catalogue are Delta tables in a lakehouse. Two Spark notebooks rewrite
  Regista's rules in SQL and recompute its numbers: **756 of 756** and **7,399 of 7,399** rows match.
- **GitHub Actions:** 1,145 tests, an image build with an in-image smoke test, and deploys with a federated identity,
  no stored secret. The app reaches Azure through Entra ID roles, with key login switched off.

## Synthetic data, by design

The rules ask for synthetic, football-realistic data, so Regista makes its own: a full league season plus 13 more
competitions.
- The events include substitutions, penalties, aerial duels, cards and offsides.
- Every player moves at 10 Hz within their own pace, and a broadcast renderer gives exact ground truth.
- The league sits inside Premier League per-90 and physical bands, and each catalogue level is checked only against
  what a published source supports.
- Every person, club and competition is invented.
- The vision layer is measured on matches it never saw: clip recall 0.75, precision 0.94, on rendered footage.

## Category

**Best Multi-Agent System.** Distinct agent roles, shared state and typed handoffs, recovery from failure, and a human
in the loop, across two Microsoft Agent Framework workflows plus Foundry agents with MCP tools.
