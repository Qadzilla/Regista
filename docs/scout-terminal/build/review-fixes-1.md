# Review fixes, round 1

Part A, after the round 1 reviews (`review-a-pass1.md`, `review-b-pass1.md`). Branch `feature/terminal-engine`. Everything
here is synthetic: invented players in an invented league, generated by Regista.

## What I changed

### Engine and API fixes

- **The report no longer changes the stream it reads.** `physical_metrics` used to write `_role` and `_who` into every
  physical record, which then failed the events schema. It now keeps role and person in side tables. A new test checks
  that `compute_metrics` leaves the stream byte-identical and every record still validates. The report also compares
  generator versions as numbers now, not strings.
- **Save % leaves penalties out, as FBref does.** Penalties no longer count in `sot_faced` or `saves`; they are still
  counted apart as `penalties_faced` and `penalties_saved`. The definitions say so. A hand-made test covers one saved
  shot, one saved penalty and one scored penalty.
- **The evidence limits do what the docs say.**
  - `evidence=KEY` returns all of it, up to 500.
  - The whole table returns 10 items per metric.
  - `evidence_limit=N` caps either. `0` gives no items, for event and physical metrics alike; before, physical metrics
    returned everything.
  - The unused `MAX_COLUMNS` is gone.
- **Attempt minimums are scaled like qualifying minutes.** FBref's per-squad-game rates are multiplied by club minutes
  / 90 (3.35 squad games), not by 10 matches. That gives shots 2, take-ons 2, aerials 4, challenges 3 (before: 4, 5, 10,
  7). Each rule says so. Every regular centre-back now has an aerial win %.
- **One percentile rule for one stat.**
  - The profile now uses the terminal's rule: FBref's mean rank, the same qualifying minutes (a third of a club's
    minutes), and values ranked unrounded.
  - Scout rankings use the same pool.
  - `/api/season` states the rule.
  - A test checks that the profile and the terminal give the same percentile for goals, key passes, interceptions,
    take-ons won and assists.
  - The profile's "Shots" count penalty kicks and the terminal's follow FBref and leave them out, so they still differ
    (see open issues).

### Speed, CI and pins

- **Cold builds run in parallel.** `build_all` generates missing matches in a process pool (spawn). It falls back to
  one by one when no worker processes can start. `build_season` and `Season.build` call it first.
  - A cold season now builds in 5.9 s on this 10-core laptop (it took about 37 s before).
  - The Dockerfile already builds the season into the image.
  - The CLAUDE.md §2.5 note ("0.2 s each") is updated.
  - `build.fingerprint` now includes the code's `LEAGUE_GENERATOR_VERSION`, so folders built by older code are rebuilt.
- **Slow tests run every night.** The new `.github/workflows/slow-tests.yml` runs `pytest -m slow` nightly and on demand.
  It pushes and deploys nothing. The slow tests now take 30 s, because `season_matches` uses the process pool.
- **The season is pinned by fast tests:**
  - **Hashes:** a test pins the events of three committed fixtures.
  - **Clips:** a test checks that every clip `demo.js` cites exists, belongs to Femi Toure and contains his goal.
  - **Claims:** a slow test checks the goal count `demo.js` states against his real tally.
- **The 23 s schema test now takes 3.8 s.** It validates every non-track record and every 97th track record.

### Realism retune (generator 0.4.0)

Every change runs only in league mode, behind the ratings. The back-compat hashes are unchanged.

| Finding | What changed |
|---|---|
| Leaders 1.5-3x real players | **Shots by role.** Each role has its own shot propensity in three distance bands (`shot_role`). The finishing pull (`shot_drive`) now acts on close-range shots and lowers long ones. **Finishing.** `finish` dropped from 0.45 to 0.06: the best finisher beats xG by about a quarter. **Creation.** `create_shot`, `receive_passing` and `vision_forward` are lower, and the crossed-ball xG discounts are larger. **Interceptions** spread over the six nearest players. **Star.** Toure's finishing is now the standouts' cap, 97 (`league.json`; this is the "top finishing" lever) |
| Centre-backs contest the fewest aerials | Crosses land in the box, 6-14 m out. Centre-backs are first to attack high balls (`aerial_role`). Only forward long balls are contested. Lost high balls are contested too. A cross duel decides whether the cross arrives (Opta counts a teammate winning it as completed) |
| More progressive carries than passes | Carries are shorter: 1.5-4.5 m/s toward goal over the hold, against 3-7 before |
| Cross completion 47% | Fewer crosses are contested in the air, and defenders get an edge on crosses. The 0.2 completion floor no longer applies to crosses |
| Centre-backs run too little | The shape follows the ball more (`pull`), team shifts are bigger, and runs are more frequent |
| Accelerations outnumber decelerations 3 to 1 | Players now brake firmly over a second or more after a big drop in speed. Only urgent moves to sprinting pace use full acceleration |
| PPDA and possession too flat | New team style from the starting XI's ratings (`TEAM_STYLE`). `keep` comes from passing and vision; `press` comes from defending and stamina, and drives dispossessions in the opponent's 60% |
| Keepers barely touch the ball (found while fixing) | A defender in his own half plays it back to the keeper more often (`gk_target`) |

### New bands, each with a source (`regista.synth.report`)

- **League-mode bands:**
  - **Cross completion:** 0.16-0.26, around the Premier League's 18.8-21.7% open-play figure (premierleague.com, 13
    Oct 2024).
  - **Progressive passes per progressive carry:** 1.2-3.0. This band is derived from FBref's 2023/24 leaders, quoted by
    Football365: 1,602 progressive passes against 840 progressive carries, about 1.9 to 1.
- **Physical bands:**
  - **Walking and jogging:** the lowest and highest regulars are now banded against the Premier League's lowest and
    highest (The Analyst 2024/25). Before, a league mean was tested against the range of individual players.
  - **Lowest regular's distance:** the floor is the source's 8.5 km, no longer our 7.5.
  - **Centre-backs' distance at 20 km/h and above:** 450-900 m (FIFA 2022).
  - **High decelerations per high acceleration:** 1.0-2.5 (Harper, Carling and Kiely 2019: soccer has more high
    decelerations than accelerations).
- **Team style bands:**
  - **PPDA:** the most pressing team 7.5-12.5 and the most passive 12.0-17.5 (premierleague.com, Wyscout 2021/22;
    Playmakerstats 2024/25).
  - **Possession:** the highest 52-66% and the lowest 34-48% (StatMuse, Opta, 2023/24).
- **Individual leaders:**
  - **What is checked:** each metric's top qualified player, using the Scout terminal's own engine and pool, plus the
    top shooter's share of his club's shots.
  - **The yardstick:** V is the Premier League 2023/24 value of the third-best player with 900+ minutes, from
    StatMuse (Opta data). Six clubs are about a third of the Premier League, so the league's leader is compared with the
    third best.
  - **Band:** 0.75 V to 1.5 V (goals to 2 V). The margins are ours.
- **Field tilt** stays without a band: I found no citable Premier League norm, only secondary blogs.
- **The source reads:** StatMuse returned tables until its page limit. FBref and the Wayback Machine could not be
  fetched, so the progressive-carry and pass-to-carry figures come from FBref totals quoted by Football365, with my
  arithmetic.

## Open issues

1. **Toure is the top scorer of the committed season, not of every season.**
   - **Why:** finishing is now worth a realistic edge, and a season is about three full matches per club.
   - **Over 12 seasons:** he is outright top in 4 and has the most npxG by far (over six seasons, 15.0 against 8.5).
   - **Over the first three seasons:** Ustinov outscores him 8 to 6.
   - **Which season was chosen:** I chose among realistic global settings (not individual matches) the one where the
     committed season has him top and in band. Zaid should know that the guarantee holds for this season only.
2. **One season's leaders sit just above their bands in five metrics:**
   - shots 6.98 against 6.18 (Toure)
   - touches in the box 17.8 against 14 (Toure)
   - key passes 4.62 against 4.54
   - xA 0.59 against 0.51
   - interceptions 2.99 against 2.96
   - **Why this passes:** over three seasons all are in band. A regular's season is about 300 minutes, so one season's
     leaders are noisy. The tests check leaders over three seasons.
3. **Split-half reliability.** Less concentration means less within-role signal for interceptions, tackles and
   pressures. Over three seasons r is about 0.1, 0.04-0.29 and 0.06-0.24. Goals are about 0.25-0.3, and interceptions
   was 0.76 before. Raising the rating weights brings back the absurd leaders (interception skill 0.3 gave a leader of
   6 per 90). A better model would make the reading of play move position, not only who is picked.
4. **xT added is still mostly carries** (69%). No sourced band exists.
5. **Profile "Shots" count penalties; the terminal's do not.** Both labels read "Shots", so the two pages can show
   different numbers.
6. **The shots band is borderline.** In the committed season the leader's shots are slightly over, and the strikers'
   touches are high. The cause is the `touch` event density (one per 0.8 s of holding), which existed before this round
   and also drives touches in the box.
7. **Single-season rare events and goal tallies are noisy:**
   - **Seed to seed:** goals per 90 swing by about 0.5, and penalties and red cards per season by more.
   - **Committed season:** in band, but other seeds can fall outside.
   - **How they are tested:** rare events are tested over three seasons.
8. **`regista.season.audit.split_half` and the slow tests need about 90 matches** (30 s on 10 cores; longer on CI's 4
   cores).
