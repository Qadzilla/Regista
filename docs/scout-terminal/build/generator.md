# Build report: the generator uses the players' ratings (league mode)

Phase 2 of part A, 2026-10-23, branch `feature/terminal-engine`. All data here is synthetic: invented players in an
invented league, generated by Regista.

## What I built

**League mode in the generator** (`src/regista/synth/generator.py`). When both squads carry the hidden ratings from
the attributes phase, the match runs in league mode (generator version 0.2.0, profile `league`). Without ratings,
nothing changes: every random draw happens in the same order as before, and the output is byte-identical to `main`.

How ratings move outcomes: each rating becomes a skill score, `z = (rating - centre) / 7`. The centre is roughly the
rating of the average league player who performs that action. The score is added in log-odds, with weights in the
`LEAGUE` table, so league-wide rates stay where the profile was tuned.

| Outcome | Ratings involved |
|---|---|
| pass completion | passing, plus vision on a through ball, against the defending of the defender nearest where the ball lands (and of the marker, under pressure) |
| chance creation | vision: forward runners are picked more often, forward balls into the final third lead the receiver into space, through balls are tried more often, and a pass from a player with vision sets up a shot more often |
| who gets the ball | teammates look for good passers, and in the final third for good finishers |
| shot outcome | finishing (aerial for headers), strong or weak foot, against the keeper's shot stopping. xG stays the chance's quality, so goals minus xG is now a real finishing signal |
| save held or parried | the keeper's handling (a parry gives a rebound) |
| take-ons | dribbling against defending; good dribblers try more, and nutmeg and skill more |
| tackles, interceptions, fouls | defending: winning the ball, reading a loose pass (the better of the two nearest defenders tends to get there), fouling less |
| carries | dribbling |
| aerial duels | aerial rating and height (per 10 cm), with a small edge to the defender |
| who comes off | stamina |
| foot and body part | preferred foot: 82% of passes and 80% of shots with the strong foot (95% of penalties); 50/50 for two-footed players |

**New events, contract 0.2.0.** These live in `data/synthetic/README.md` ("League mode") and
`src/regista/schemas/regista-events.schema.json`. The new event type is `aerial_duel`, and possession `lost_by` gains
`offside`. Every 0.1.x stream stays valid.

- `substitution`: 2-5 per team (weights give a mean of 3.9), made at stoppages after 58-95% of the match. The player
  coming off is weighted toward low stamina. A bench player of the same role, or failing that the same line, comes on.
  Keepers are never substituted.
- `penalty`: given for a foul whose victim is in the box. Most contact in the box is not given: the defender wins the
  ball cleanly 80% of the time. The taker is his team's best finisher on the pitch. The kick is a `shot` with the
  qualifier `penalty` and xG 0.79 (FBref/Opta's figure).
- `aerial_duel`: one record per contest, on crosses, long balls and long goal kicks.
- `card_red`: second yellows and rare straight reds. The player leaves and his team plays with ten. Keepers are exempt.
- `offside`: a mistimed run on a through ball, or a forward pass to the player furthest forward. It is followed by an
  indirect free kick.
- **Through balls are real.** Before this they were tagged 0 times in 9,919 passes; now about 3 per 90.
- **Key passes count every shot.** Before, only passes before a goal or a save were tagged. Now the completed pass that
  started the shooter's possession is tagged for any outcome, and a goal makes it the assist.
- Saves carry `held` or `parried`. Long goal kicks carry the qualifier `goal_kick`.

**Schema stamping.** `SCHEMA_VERSION` is now 0.2.0, and `LEGACY_SCHEMA_VERSION` is 0.1.1. The generator stamps a match
0.2.0 only in league mode. Clips carry the version of the stream they were cut from, so the committed samples' clips
are unchanged.

**Clip rule and labeller** (`regista.clips`):

- A substitution is not a moment (`NOT_MOMENTS`). It never appears among a clip's events or tags.
- An aerial duel tags the winner's clip `aerial_duel` (weight 0.35, the same as a block).
- Other new tags: `penalty_won` 0.6, `card_red` 0.5, `through_ball` 0.5 and `offside` 0.1. `penalty` (0.8) was already
  in the table.

**Profiles and the league build:**

- Minutes now count time on the pitch, through a new module, `regista.season.playing_time`, shared with the build and
  the Fabric lineups.
- Substitutes who came on get an appearance, minutes and their own clips.
- New stats: aerial duels won and lost, aerial win %, through balls, offsides, penalties won and penalty goals. Lost
  duels are credited through `related_player_id`.
- The Fabric notebook now sums `lineups.played_s` instead of counting starters.

**Realism report** (`python -m regista.synth.report --league [--repeat N]`):

- It regenerates the league in memory and checks the 13 existing bands.
- League-mode streams also get 5 new bands, each with its source (table below), plus diagnostics without a band.

**Audit** (`python -m regista.season.audit [--seasons N]`): repeats the split-half test from `regista-data.md` and
prints the scoring chart.

**Back-compat:**

- The friendlies filmed for the Player view call `generate_kwargs(..., ratings=False)`, so their footage keeps the
  format the vision layer was trained on.
- `tests/test_backcompat.py` still pins 8 no-ratings runs and the committed syn_0294 files against the hashes recorded
  on `main` e3da71d. Its league-shaped case now pins `main`'s league settings (profile "realistic"), because the league
  file now names the profile "league".

## How I checked it

- **Back-compat:** all 9 back-compat tests pass. The samples, vision seeds 201/202/301, realistic seed 77 and the
  league-shaped run without ratings are byte-identical to `main`.
- **Determinism:** the same league file and seed give the same events and tracks (a test generates one fixture twice
  and compares hashes).
- **Statistics:**
  - The committed season (30 fixtures, about 904 minutes) is reported below.
  - So is a pool of 6 seasons: the same fixtures with every seed shifted by 0, 1000, ..., 5000; 180 matches, 5,421
    minutes. Rare events need the pool.
  - Before trusting the finishing model, I checked it was unbiased. Over 6 fresh seasons (seeds 3000-8000), the goals
    the model expected and the goals that happened differed by z = 0.17. An earlier excess, seen only in seasons
    0-2000, was chance.
- **Tests:**
  - New: `tests/test_league_mode.py`, with 14 fast tests (about 15-30 s) and 3 slow ones (`-m slow`, about 1 minute).
  - Updated: back-compat, attributes (the squads-and-fixtures hash is now checked against `main`'s league file; the
    generator block changed on purpose), synthetic (0.1.1 stamp), fabric, season, product page and shared dashboard.
  - Slow tests are deselected by default (`addopts = "-m 'not slow'"` in pyproject.toml).

## Open issues

1. **Foundry summaries were replaced by rules summaries.**
   - Every player's numbers changed, so `data/season/summaries.json` had to be rewritten.
   - Tests and this phase never reach Azure, so I rewrote it with the rules engine (`python -m regista.season
     summarise --fresh`, model rules-v0).
   - To restore Foundry-written summaries before a deploy, run `python -m regista.season summarise --cloud`. It needs
     `az login` and spends Foundry quota.
   - `summaries.py` gained one fallback sentence for players with too few minutes to rank (substitutes with a few
     minutes).
2. **The demo seeds were re-pinned** (`src/regista/static/shared/demo.js`, Toure's clip references and the seeded
   notes) to the regenerated season, and every claim was re-checked against it:
   - 9 goals and top scorer; most shots on target per 90 among the 18 regular forwards
   - 2 of 7 take-ons won
   - Ustinov third for goals per 90
   - Mensah among the top defenders for key passes (87th percentile)
   - two goals against Ashby in round 5 and against Calder in round 7

   `docs/demo-storyboard.md` now says nine goals; the reel's length will differ from the 44 s it noted.
3. **The star's rate is not realistic as an individual number.** Toure scores about 2.8 goals per 90, which is video-game
   level (the Premier League's best is about 1). A 30-match season of 30-minute matches is about ten full matches, so a
   top scorer chosen by finishing alone needs a large edge to come first reliably. League totals stay in band. Shorter
   seasons are the root cause; longer matches would make individual rates honest.
4. **Ratings on low-volume actions** (tackles, aerial duels, take-on success) show only weakly within one season. The
   top aerial win rates in the committed season belong to full-backs with 15-20 duels, not the centre-back standout.
   Small samples; the pooled figures show the signal.
5. **Physical ratings are unused.** Top speed and acceleration stay for the physical phase. Stamina only picks who comes
   off; it does not yet tire players late in a match.
6. **Sources for three new bands are secondary or old.** Offsides come from a club site and Anfield Index; aerial
   duels come from an older season via Anfield Index. They are labelled as such in `report.py`. Through balls,
   cross completion and yellow cards have no band. Cross completion (0.46) is well above Opta's typical 22-25%, which
   I did not source.
7. **Built match folders do not know the code version.** A match's fingerprint covers the league file, not the
   generator code. When generator tuning changes, `data/season/matches` must be deleted and rebuilt, as I did. The live
   app builds on first request, so a new image starts clean.
8. **Vision outputs are now stamped 0.2.0**, and so are new annotations, because they use `SCHEMA_VERSION`. That is
   valid (0.2.0 is a superset), but those streams have none of the new events. Only the generator keeps the 0.1.1 stamp,
   for byte identity.
9. Penalties are never won by keepers, keepers are never sent off, and there are no injuries or VAR. Offsides are
   decided by a probability, not geometry: the shapes never put an attacker behind the second-last defender (0 of 1,638
   forward passes measured).
