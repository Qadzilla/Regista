# Build report: physical metrics that are real (league mode, motion at 10 Hz)

Phase 3 of part A, 2026-10-23, branch `feature/terminal-engine`. All data here is synthetic: invented players in an
invented league, generated by Regista. Physical numbers are the simulator's own motion, metered exactly (no tracking
noise, no smoothing).

## What I built

**Motion (generator 0.3.0, league mode only).** In league mode the generator now moves all 22 players on the pitch
every 0.1 s, not only the ones the camera sees, each within his own `top_speed_kmh` and `acceleration_ms2`
(`src/regista/synth/generator.py`, `_advance_league` and `_move_league`, constants in `MOTION`).

- **Kinematics.** Each step a player's velocity turns toward the speed and direction he wants. Acceleration is his
  rating up to two thirds of top speed, then tapers to zero at top speed. Unhurried moves speed up gently (1.4 m/s²);
  braking is 1.4 times his acceleration. He never goes above his top speed.
- **What he wants to do.**
  - Off the ball he follows his shape position, which follows where the ball has been over the last 1.5 s, plus a slow
    personal drift. He walks when near it and runs when far.
  - The nearest defender closes the holder down. Just after a turnover a second man joins the press.
  - Attackers make runs toward goal, and the defender nearest the space they are running into goes with them.
  - After a turnover, players upfield of their shape recover: defenders flat out when the ball is in their half, at
    hard running pace otherwise.
  - Receivers run to where the pass will arrive. Chasers run to loose balls, clearances and balls in behind, and the
    keeper goes for shots.
  - At a dead ball everyone walks back, and the set-piece taker walks to the spot.
- **Fatigue.** Sprints drain a reserve that only slow running refills, so repeated sprints get shorter. Willingness
  and top speed fade over the match, less for players with high `stamina`.
- **Ball at feet.** The carrier now runs where his legs take him and the ball stays with him. Before, the ball moved
  in a straight line and the carrier was pinned to it.
- **Fewer teleports.** League mode no longer teleports players. Receivers of through balls and lead passes, loose-ball
  winners and clearance takers run to the ball (`_reach`); set-piece takers walk to the spot during the dead ball
  (`_set_piece`). The jumps that remain are logged as `relocated_m` and never counted as distance: a keeper's dive,
  the last metres to a spot, the reset after a goal. They come to about 23 m per 90 against 9.6 km run.
- **Separate random stream.** Motion uses its own random stream (`move_rng`), so the 22 players' drift never reorders
  the match's own draws.

**New record type `physical` (schema 0.2.0).** One per player who played, appended at the end of the stream.
`src/regista/synth/physical.py` holds the meters (`Body`) and `DEFINITIONS`, which gives each metric its definition and
source row in `industry-metrics.md`. The record carries:

- distance
- FIFA speed zones 1-5 (0-6.9, 7-14.9, 15-19.9, 20-24.9 and 25+ km/h)
- high-intensity distance (≥ 20 km/h) and sprint distance (≥ 25 km/h)
- walking and jogging time (The Analyst's 6 and 15 km/h bands)
- high-intensity efforts and sprints (≥ 1 s, Wyscout)
- top speed
- medium and high accelerations and decelerations (Wyscout's 1.5 and 3 m/s², held at least 1 s)
- `relocated_m`
- per-period distance and high-intensity distance, and the method (10 Hz and the thresholds)

The measured top speed is named `peak_speed_kmh`, so the hidden rating's name `top_speed_kmh` never reaches public
JSON. The schema, `regista.schema.EVENT_TYPES` and `data/synthetic/README.md` document the record.

**Tracks are unchanged in format.** They are still sampled at `track_hz` (1 Hz for the league) from the same positions,
for the renderer and the vision layer. I did not write full 10 Hz tracks: that would be about 400,000 records per
match, against about 35,000 today, so it is not cheap. The summary record is what the metrics phase needs.

**Realism report** (`python -m regista.synth.report --league [--repeat N]`). It has 11 new physical bands, each with
its source, plus diagnostics. `regista.synth.report.physical_metrics` computes them.

**Audit.** `python -m regista.season.audit` adds split-half reliability for distance, high-intensity distance, sprint
distance, sprints and top speed.

**Generator 0.3.0.** `LEAGUE_GENERATOR_VERSION` and `league.json` both say 0.3.0, so every league match's fingerprint
changed and the server rebuilds stale match folders by itself.

**Retuning, because players now move differently, so event rates moved too.**

- League profile shot weights: (16, 0.135, 24, 0.12, 32, 0.085). More shots come from distance because attackers no
  longer glide into the box at 25 km/h.
- `lose` 0.90 and `foul_on_tackle` 0.34.
- Finishing centre 75 (it was 78.5), so league goals sit near xG. `finish` weight 0.45 (it was 0.35), so finishing
  decides more and Toure stays the star.
- `box_foul_given` 0.22, `aerial_reach_m` 8.5, `straight_red` 0.004.
- League bookings: 17% of fouls, 8% for a player already booked.

## Open issues

- **Distances sit in the lower half of the bands.** The league averages 9.7 km against the Premier League's typical
  10-11 km, and the lowest regular, a centre-back, runs 7.9 km against the PL's 8.5. Pushing distance up by pulling
  defenders toward the ball broke the event bands (goals fell to 1.2 per 90), so I left it.
- **Matches are 30 minutes.** Per-match peaks over a third of a match sit about 1-2 km/h below FIFA's full-match
  figures for central roles (CB 27.7 against 30.2-30.8). Per-90 rates are honest; per-match maxima are not
  full-match maxima.
- **Accelerations have no band.** Neither do high accelerations (about 68 per 90) or decelerations (about 21), because
  no source in `industry-metrics.md` gives a norm. High-intensity efforts (about 46 per 90) have none either.
  Decelerations are low against accelerations because players brake gently near their targets; that is worth
  revisiting with a source.
- **The sprints band is derived, not published.** No source gives a league mean for ≥ 25 km/h held for 1 s. The
  jogging band's lower bound is ours.
- **Some sources are aggregated or secondary.** World Cup figures are team totals divided by about ten outfield
  players. HSR-REV pools studies with different thresholds.
- **Goalkeepers have no band.** They run about 4.0 km per 90. The Analyst's Raya walked 114.9 km over the season, about
  4 km a match.
- **Second-half drops are noisy within one season.** High-intensity drop is 0.09 in the pool but ranged 0.02-0.13
  across single-season runs while I tuned.
- **Toure's rate is far above any real player's.** He scores 3.2 goals per 90 in this season. A season is about ten
  full matches, so the star needs a big edge.
- **Rare events and Toure's lead move with any change.** Retuning anything in league mode re-rolls the committed
  season, because play is chaotic. I checked the committed season and a 6-season pool after the final change. Future
  phases must do the same: `python3 -m regista.synth.report --league`, `--repeat 6`, and `python3 -m regista.season
  audit`.
- **Single-season ratios are still weak.** Take-on success, aerial duels won and pass completion repeat only weakly
  within one season. They show over 3 seasons.
- **Built league folders.** Run `rm -rf data/season/matches && python3 -m regista.season build` after generator
  changes. The version bump makes the server rebuild on its own.
- **Foundry summaries.** Summaries are rules-engine only. Restore Foundry summaries before a deploy with
  `python -m regista.season summarise --cloud`.
