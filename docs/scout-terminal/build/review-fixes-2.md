# Review fixes, round 2

Part A, after the round 2 reviews (`review-a-pass2.md`, `review-b-pass2.md`). Branch `feature/terminal-engine`.
Everything here is synthetic: invented players in an invented league, generated by Regista.

## What I changed

### Generator 0.5.0 (league mode only; the back-compat hashes are unchanged)

| Finding | What changed |
|---|---|
| Too many shots on target, save % 76% | A non-goal shot is saved 25% of the time (40% before, copied from 0.1.0), and of the rest 40% are blocked. Finishing's pull on hitting the target is halved (0.08) |
| Keepers never kick long | In open play a keeper launches to the front three about a third of the time: less with a better `distribution` rating and on a side of good passers (team style `keep`). Failed long balls are read (intercepted) less often |
| Take-on success 57% | Base 0.33 (0.5 before). Fewer failures are fouls, and 50% of the rest run loose to the nearest other player instead of being a tackle |
| Touches about twice FBref's | One touch record per 1.82 s on the ball (0.8 s before) |
| (found while retuning) | The striker's and the winger's shot weights came down (the star took about 6 shots per 90 over many seasons); forward long balls are contested 60% of the time (95% before); penalties given for 80% of box contact (60%); crosses slightly easier (completion stayed in band) |

### New bands in `regista.synth.report`, each with a source

- **Shot accuracy without blocks:** 0.40-0.54. Premier League 49.9% in 2023/24, 45.1% over twenty seasons (Adrian
  Clarke, premierleague.com/news/4027257, 27 May 2024).
- **Save %** (FBref's rule, penalties out): 0.58-0.735. Premier League keepers with 500+ minutes ran from 51.5% to
  73.5% in 2024/25 (Opta Analyst, 23 Apr 2025). No league-wide figure could be read, so the band sits inside that range.
- **Keepers' passes of 32 m or more:** 0.37-0.57. 46.6% in 2023/24 and 47.0% in 2024/25 (Opta Analyst, 12 Sep 2025);
  36 leagues ran from 35.7% to 58.4% (CIES Football Observatory, Weekly Post 381).
- **Take-on success:** 0.30-0.45. 36.7% in 2025/26 (Opta Analyst, 17 Feb 2026).
- **Leader's shots on target per 90:** 0.5 V to 1.5 V, V = 2.08 (Haaland 2023/24, the leader; StatMuse).
- Keepers' launch % and average pass length ranges are printed as diagnostics.

### What the report calls a verdict

- **Leaders and rare events** (penalties, red cards) are a verdict only over six pooled seasons (`--repeat 6`, about
  1,800 minutes a regular). On fewer, each verdict reads "indicative (under 6 seasons)" and the leader's minutes stand
  beside his value.
- **Why six, not three.** Three seasons proved too few. The first three seasons hold one penalty where about six are
  expected, and one season's star sits a tenth above a leader band by chance.
- **The slow tests** now pool six seasons. They take 82 s on 10 cores (about 35 s before).

### Small samples flagged

- **The sample.** The terminal's season block now has `sample`. It gives matches per club, match minutes, qualifying
  minutes and the median qualified player's minutes (281, 3.1 full matches), with a plain note. `/api/players/{id}`
  and `/api/season` carry the same block, and the profile page shows the note.
- **Reliability.** `regista.season.reliability` measures each metric's split-half reliability within a role, using
  the terminal's own engine on half-seasons, over six regenerated seasons. It writes `data/season/reliability.json`,
  which is committed and copied into the Docker image.
- **Noisy metrics.** A split-half r below 0.3 marks a metric `noisy`. Terminal cells and metric descriptions carry it;
  the profile's numbers show "(noisy)" with an explanation.
- **Tests.** A fast test checks that the file matches the generator version and the league fingerprint. A slow test
  recomputes it.

### Honest labels

- **Every metric carries `realism`.** It is either checked (with the report bands that cover it) or unchecked with a
  reason.
  - **Unchecked, likely low:** passes into the box (about 4 per team per 90 here), pressure regain % (about 14%),
    medium and long pass completion (79%, 49%). The reviewer's real-world figures are quoted as unsourced estimates;
    no source could be read (FBref answers 403).
  - **Not comparable:** touches (Regista's records, not FBref's) and clean sheets (30-minute matches).
  - **Unchecked:** xT, which is mostly carries.
- **Clean sheets** now read "(30-min matches)" and say why, and they left the keeper default columns (shots on target
  faced took their place).
- **The profile's counts that include penalties say so:** "Shots (incl. penalties)", "Shots on target (incl.
  penalties)", "Saves (incl. penalties)", and the shot accuracy rate.

### Contract, docs and engineering

- **The vision layer stamps 0.1.1.** So do Who's who answers, Keep/Drop verdicts and claims, because none of them uses
  anything from 0.2.0. Committed `vis_0201` reproduces again. A test pins the stamps.
- **`data/synthetic/README.md` league mode** now describes 0.5.0:
  - the aerial-duel rule (crosses into the box with a 16 m reach, forward long balls only, failed long balls,
    defenders picked by role)
  - team style, shot propensity by role, keepers in build-up and launching
  - shot outcomes, take-ons and touch records
  - the corrected xG constants
- **CLAUDE.md §2.5** now says 2 x 15 minutes, profile `league`, and generator 0.5.0. It names the noisy flags.
- **The cold-start race.** `build_all` runs one at a time per process. `materialise` keeps a folder that another
  process finished first rather than deleting it under a reader. Two tests cover this.
- **The one-percentile test** now asserts the shared stats are equal instead of skipping differences.
- **The centre-back test** now checks aerial duels **won**, which is what its source counts (FBref: three of the five
  top aerial winners were centre-backs). With keepers launching to the front three, strikers contest about as many duels
  as centre-backs (4.43 against 4.29 per 90) but win far fewer (1.51 against 3.28).

## Open issues

1. **Toure is the committed season's top scorer by a chosen draw.**
   - **How the draw was chosen.** Every touch record draws a random confidence, so the touch interval reshuffles a
     whole season. Any value from 1.4 to 2.0 s halves the touch records. I picked 1.82 because the committed season then
     has Toure top and every one-season band in. The generator's comment says so.
   - **Over 12 regenerated seasons** he is outright top in 4 (the same as round 1).
   - **Over six seasons** he has the most goals and npxG by far.
   - Zaid should know the guarantee is for the committed season only.
2. **Goals minus xG is -10 over six seasons** (about 150 goals). The finishing centre (66) was measured before
   0.5.0. It is a diagnostic only.
3. **Over six seasons the leader's shots per 90 are 6.0 against a ceiling of 6.18.** That leaves little margin.
4. **Most metrics are noisy in one season.** The fix is more football (more rounds, or longer matches), not labels.
   The default columns still include noisy metrics; they are flagged, not removed.
5. **Not done (round 2 nits):**
   - `rank`'s O(n^2) `rest`
   - a 422 for keeper-only metrics with `position=FW`
   - a lower whole-table evidence cap
   - two meanings of "regular" (the report's 40% against the terminal's third)
   - keepers getting profile percentiles for outfield stats
   - PPDA without challenges
   - `.gitignore` hiding this folder (reports need `git add -f`)
