# Build report: the metrics engine and its API

Phase 4 of part A, 2026-10-23, branch `feature/terminal-engine`. All data here is synthetic: invented players in an
invented league, generated by Regista. Every number below comes from the committed season (30 fixtures of 30 minutes,
generator 0.3.0).

## What I built

**`src/regista/season/metrics.py`, the engine.** It reads every league fixture's event stream and physical records and
computes **127 player metrics** and **19 team-style metrics**. Each one has a catalogue entry (`CATALOGUE`,
`TEAM_CATALOGUE`), which carries:

- the key and a plain-English label;
- a definition;
- whose rule it follows (for example "FBref progressive pass");
- the source keys from `industry-metrics.md`, with links in `SOURCES`;
- the unit;
- the kind: per 90, ratio, possession-adjusted, season peak, mean of match peaks, or total;
- whether higher is better (None marks a style marker, which is ranked but not judged);
- the positions it matters for.

For each player and each metric the engine also computes:

- the value;
- the count behind it;
- a percentile against position peers;
- the evidence: every event behind the number, with its match, time, value and the clip it sits in, plus a link that
  opens the Clip view at that moment.

Physical metrics come from one record per match, so their evidence is the per-match values. The engine keeps per-match
values (form) for every metric. It also builds team style per club and fits an xT model on the season.

It never reads the hidden ratings. Only the event streams and the public bio go in, and a test checks every endpoint's
JSON for rating keys.

Scope: league fixtures only. Claimed and uploaded matches are vision streams (a subset of 10 event types), so they stay
on the profiles. `profiles.py`, the Scout agent and the pages are unchanged.

**`src/regista/terminal_api.py`, the API.** Every endpoint is a public read, listed in `tests/test_access.py`'s access
table. Every response says the data is synthetic.

| Endpoint | What it returns |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/metrics` | the catalogue (each metric with its display `digits` and, for a ratio, `min_attempts` and its rule), the groups, the sources, the season's thresholds, the fitted xT grid and the `pitch` (size, attacking frame, the touch grid and its events) |
| `GET /api/terminal/screen` | players with chosen columns (`metrics=a,b,...`, up to 16; default: the position's core set). Filters: `position`, `role` (the 4-3-3 slot), `club`, `min_minutes` (default: the qualifying minimum), `age_min`/`age_max`, `foot`, `q`. Also `sort`, `order`, `limit`, `offset` (page past `limit`; `total` counts every row) |
| `GET /api/terminal/rank?metric=` | a leaderboard (ties share a rank). Players without enough attempts are listed separately, unranked |
| `GET /api/terminal/scatter?x=&y=` | two metrics per player, plus the pool's medians |
| `GET /api/players/{id}/metrics` | every metric (or one `group`), each with its value, count, percentile, peers, and the first `evidence_limit` (default 10) events with their total. `evidence=KEY` returns one metric's evidence in pages of up to 500 events from `evidence_offset`; page on while `offset + len(items) < total` to read every moment. Each event has its football `minute` |
| `GET /api/players/{id}/heatmap` | the player's on-ball actions per pitch cell (12 by 8, attacking left to right) in league fixtures only, like every terminal number; the profile's own grid also counts claimed and uploaded matches (the terminal's heat map) |
| `GET /api/players/{id}/shots` | every shot the player took, penalties included: attacking-frame location and end point, xG, outcome, body part, penalty, minute, round, opponent, clip (the terminal's shot map) |
| `GET /api/players/{id}/form?metrics=` | per-match values, then mean, sd, coefficient of variation, and the last 3 matches against the season value |
| `GET /api/players/{id}/similar?n=` | the most similar players in the same position |
| `GET /api/teams`, `GET /api/teams/{club_id}/style` | each club's style values, league rank, league mean, per-match values; set-piece xG has its evidence (the shots, each with its taker, minute and clip) |
| `GET /api/teams/{club_id}/shots?side=for\|against` | every shot by the club or against it in its matches, with who took it, xG, outcome, minute and clip (the terminal's team reels) |

Inputs are validated the same way as the existing endpoints:

- ids and keys are checked against patterns;
- an unknown metric or group returns 422;
- an unknown player or club returns 404;
- a malformed id returns 400;
- `age_min` above `age_max` returns 422.

**Caching.** The season is built once per process, keyed on the league file's path and mtime and the match folder
(`load_metrics`). That takes about 1.0 s on an M2 Pro once the match folders exist. `warm_season` builds it in the
background after the profiles. Once built, endpoints answer in 1-8 ms.

**CLI.** `python -m regista.season.metrics` prints league diagnostics; add `--rank KEY [--position FW]` or
`--player ID` for a leaderboard or one player.

## The metrics

The groups below follow the catalogue; the full definitions are in `GET /api/metrics`.

- **Shooting**
  - goals and non-penalty goals
  - shots and shots on target (both without penalties, as FBref counts them)
  - shot-on-target %
  - xG, npxG and npxG per shot
  - non-penalty goals minus xG
  - big chances: xG of 0.30 or more, plus every penalty. This is Regista's threshold standing in for Opta's tagger
    judgement, and the definition says so.
  - average shot distance
  - penalties taken and scored
- **Chance creation**
  - assists
  - key passes, by FBref's rule: the completed pass the shooter received at the start of the possession he shot from,
    linked through `possession_id`, penalties excluded
  - xA (FBref xAG / Wyscout xA)
  - npxG + xA
  - SCA and GCA: FBref's two offensive actions before a shot. They are live- or dead-ball passes, take-ons, shots,
    fouls drawn, tackles and interceptions, taken inside the shooting team's possession; each piece of evidence notes
    the action type.
  - through balls
  - crosses, crosses completed and cross completion
  - passes and crosses into the penalty area (set pieces excluded)
- **Passing and progression**
  - passes, passes completed and completion
  - total and progressive passing distance
  - progressive passes, by FBref's rule: at least 10 yards past the ball's furthest point in the team's last six passes,
    or any completed pass into the box. Passes from the defending 40% are excluded. Everything is measured in the
    team's attacking direction, flipped by period. The window resets when the opponent has the ball and after a
    kick-off.
  - passes into the final third
  - switches (more than 40 yards across the pitch)
  - passes under pressure and completion under pressure
  - completion by length (FBref's 5-15, 15-30 and over 30 yards)
  - completion by zone (the pass's starting third)
- **Carrying and dribbling**
  - touches
  - touches in the box: touch records there, plus the touch ending each carry into it, because Regista places a
    possession's touch records at its start
  - passes received and progressive passes received
  - carries and carry distance
  - progressive carries (FBref's rule; a carry ending in the defending half does not count) and progressive carry
    distance
  - carries into the final third and into the box
  - take-ons attempted, won and %
  - nutmegs and skill moves
  - ball losses (Wyscout)
  - fouls won, penalties won and offsides
- **Defending**
  - tackles won
  - dribbled past
  - tackle success %: tackles won / (tackles won + dribbled past). FBref counts only dribblers tackled; Regista counts
    every tackle, and the definition says so.
  - interceptions and tackles + interceptions
  - tackles + interceptions possession-adjusted: per 30 minutes of opponent possession while the player was on the
    pitch (Wyscout PAdj). Opponent possession is in-play time on the ball plus the flight of passes to a teammate, up
    to 4 s.
  - pressures, pressure regains and regain %. A regain means the team wins the ball within 5 s in open play: by
    tackle, interception, loose ball or rebound. A set piece does not count.
  - recoveries, clearances and blocks
  - aerial duels: total, won, lost and win %
  - fouls, yellow cards and red cards
- **Goalkeeping**
  - shots on target faced, goals conceded and saves
  - save %: (SoTA - GA) / SoTA
  - clean sheets, full matches and clean-sheet %
  - penalties faced and saved
  - passes, completion, launch % (over 40 yards, goal kicks excluded) and average pass length
- **Physical** (from the physical records)
  - distance
  - high-speed running (20-25 km/h)
  - high-intensity distance (20 km/h and above)
  - sprint distance and sprints
  - high-intensity runs
  - high accelerations and decelerations
  - top speed for the season (the highest match peak)
  - top speed in a typical match (the mean of match peaks, over matches with 10 or more minutes)
  - share of time walking
  - The hidden ratings' names never appear; the measured top speed is `peak_speed`.
- **Value and impact**
  - xT added, split into passes and carries
  - on-pitch goal difference and xG difference, with the for and against figures behind each (FBref +/-)
- **Team style** (per club)
  - possession: time-based (Wyscout) and pass share (FBref)
  - field tilt
  - PPDA: opponent passes in their own 60% per tackle, interception or foul there
  - direct speed: metres gained per second of open-play sequence (Opta)
  - passes per sequence and long-pass share
  - xG for and against, npxG, goals and shots for and against
  - set-piece xG and set-piece shots: within 14 s of a corner, free kick or throw-in, before the opponent has the ball
    (Wyscout)
  - penalty xG
  - high turnovers: sequences won within 40 m of goal (Opta)
  - pressures

**xT.** This is Karun Singh's model on a 12 x 8 grid in the attacking frame:

- s(z) and m(z) are the shares of actions in a zone that are shots and moves;
- T counts successful moves over all attempted moves, so failed passes and carries carry nothing;
- g(z) is the mean Regista xG of the non-penalty shots taken from that zone (a season has too few goals per zone to
  use goals);
- the model is iterated to convergence (85 iterations, on 13,191 actions).

Its values run from 0.008 in the corners to 0.22 in the central zone nearest the goal.

**Form and consistency.** Each match gives a per-90 value, a match ratio or the match's top speed. Consistency is the
coefficient of variation over matches with at least 15 minutes. This is **Regista's own measure**, labelled as such,
because no provider publishes one.

**Similar players.** For each player I take the per-90 values of FBref's summary template (18 metrics; keepers have
their own 9-metric template), z-score them within the position over qualified players, and rank by cosine similarity.
A missing ratio counts as the peer mean. Each match also lists the shared strengths (both players have z > 0.5).
FBref ranks by smallest percentile differences instead; the response says so.

## Thresholds

- **Qualifying minutes: a third of a club's season minutes, which is 100.6 here.** FBref's rate-leader rule is 30
  minutes per squad game, a third of each 90-minute match; Regista's matches last 30 minutes. 66 players qualify: 24
  DF, 18 MF, 18 FW and 6 GK, the starters. Only qualified players are in a percentile pool or get a percentile; the
  others show values with `qualified: false`.
- **Ratio minimums: FBref's "per squad game" rules multiplied by the 10 matches a club plays.**

  | Ratio | Minimum | FBref rule |
  |---|---|---|
  | shot ratios | 4 shots | 0.395 per game |
  | take-on % | 5 take-ons | 0.5 per game |
  | aerial win % | 10 aerial duels | 0.97 per game |
  | tackle success % | 7 challenges | 0.625 per game |

  FBref publishes no minimum for the others, so they are Regista's own and labelled as such: 10 attempts for
  completion rates and regain %, 10 shots on target faced for save %, and 3 full matches for clean-sheet %.
- **Percentiles: FBref's rule.** A percentile is the mean of the share of peers strictly below and the share at or
  below. It is inverted where lower is better. Peers are the league's four positions; FBref uses six sets, but six
  squads are too small for that.

## How I checked it

**Unit tests on hand-made streams** (`tests/test_metrics.py`, 37 tests, about 5 s):

- Progressive passes:
  - exactly 10 yards counts, and just under does not;
  - the furthest point of the last six passes stops a pass that only regains ground, and a seventh pass pushes it out
    of the window;
  - passes from the defending 40% are excluded;
  - a 5 m pass into the box counts;
  - the second half is flipped;
  - failed passes never count.
- Progressive carries: a carry ending in the defending half is excluded.
- Key passes: a key pass links to the received pass; a loose-ball shot and a penalty get none.
- SCA: credits the two actions before the shot (foul drawn and take-on) and stops at the opponent's possession.
- PPDA: counts only the zone's passes and actions.
- Pressure regains: 4.5 s counts; 6 s and a throw-in do not.
- Possession adjustment: includes pass flight time.
- Keepers: shots on target faced, saves, goals conceded and clean sheets.
- The FBref percentile, the thresholds and a toy xT model.
- Every catalogue entry has a definition and a known source, and no key collides with a hidden rating.

**The engine on three real fixtures:**

- Key passes equal the generator's `key_pass` tags: 256 of 256 over the committed season.
- Saves + goals conceded = shots on target faced.
- Progressive passes made = progressive passes received.
- Each match's two possession shares add to 100.
- xT rises toward goal.
- Evidence items point at real events of the right type and player, with clip URLs.

**API:**

- the shape of every endpoint;
- 19 validation cases;
- the position, foot, age, role and club filters;
- no hidden rating key anywhere in 9 payloads.

**Full suite:** `python3 -m pytest -q` passed (count in the commit message). This phase changed no generator code, so
the back-compat hashes and the realism bands are untouched.

## Open issues

1. **Progressive carries look high.**
   - The league has 60 per team per 90, against 36 progressive passes, and carries make up 92% of the xT added.
   - My recollection (not verified, so not banded) is that Premier League sides on FBref had fewer progressive carries
     than progressive passes.
   - The generator's carries almost always go forward: 3,498 carries average 11.3 m, of which 10.7 m is toward goal.
     It is worth sourcing a norm and retuning carry direction in league mode.
2. **Small peer pools.**
   - Six clubs give 18 qualified forwards and midfielders, 24 defenders and 6 keepers.
   - Ratio pools are smaller still: only one defender has 10 or more aerial duels, so aerial win % has no percentile
     for defenders this season.
   - Percentiles move in steps of about 3-6 points.
3. **Positions are the league's four.** Wingers sit with strikers (FW), and full-backs with centre-backs (DF).
   `role` is a filter on every list, but percentiles do not use it.
4. **Event-model limits carry through.**
   - Shots on target leave out last-line blocks (Regista has no goal-line flag).
   - Every tackle wins the ball, so tackle success is tackles / (tackles + dribbled past).
   - Pressures are one per possession (nearest opponent within 3.5 m), so pressure counts sit below what StatsBomb
     logs.
   - Whether a won loose pass is labelled an interception or a recovery is the generator's choice, not a geometric
     test.
5. **xT and xG are circular.** xT is fitted on this season's own actions, with goal probability from Regista's xG,
   which is the generator's chance quality. They describe the simulator's world; they are not an outside model.
6. **Form is noisy.** Matches last 30 minutes, so per-match per-90 values swing widely (Toure's non-penalty goals per
   90 run 0-9 by match). The consistency figures are labelled as Regista's measure.
7. **Not wired yet.**
   - No MCP tools serve the metrics (the Scout agent still uses `search_players`/`get_profile`/`compare_players`).
   - Profiles do not show them.
   - Fabric tables do not carry them.
   - The terminal front end (part B) is not built.
8. **The live app** builds the metrics on first use after the profiles (warm thread). A cold replica's first terminal
   request waits for the season build (about 35 s if match folders are missing, about 1 s once they exist).
