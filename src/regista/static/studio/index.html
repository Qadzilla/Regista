Who's who

The identity agent's least certain calls. Answers correct identity frame by frame, recompute possession, events and clips, and become training data for the kit-number reader (Training → Learn from Who's who answers).

Claims

"That's me": someone added a match on a player's profile and said which player in it they are. Confirm and Regista runs the workflow on that match for the claimed player (clips, labels, then the Profile agent adds the match to the profile); reject and it never counts. Synthetic league players only. Check the kit number and the team against the league player, and watch a clip before you confirm.
